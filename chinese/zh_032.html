🍂 暮江吟 🍂

白居易 • Chinese & English Final Exam Study Guide

Original Poem

一道残阳铺水中，
半江瑟瑟半江红。
可怜九月初三夜，
露似真珠月似弓。

Yí dào cán yáng pū shuǐ zhōng
Bàn jiāng sè sè bàn jiāng hóng
Kě lián jiǔ yuè chū sān yè
Lù sì zhēn zhū yuè sì gōng

白话文 (Modern Chinese)

傍晚时分，夕阳洒在江面上，把江水照得一半呈碧绿色，一半映成红色。到了农历九月初三的夜晚，晶莹的露珠像珍珠一样闪亮，弯弯的新月像一张弓，美丽极了。

English Translation

The setting sun spreads across the river, making half the water emerald green and half glowing red. On the evening of the third day of the ninth lunar month, the dew sparkles like pearls, and the crescent moon looks like a bow.

Line-by-Line Explanation

LineChineseEnglish
一道残阳铺水中夕阳照在江面。The sunset shines across the river.
半江瑟瑟半江红江水一半碧绿，一半红色。Half the river is green, half is red.
可怜九月初三夜这里的“可怜”意思是“可爱”。"Lovely" rather than "pitiful".
露似真珠月似弓露珠像珍珠，月亮像弯弓。Dew is like pearls; the moon is like a bow.

中心思想 (Central Idea)

Chinese: 诗人通过夕阳、江水、露珠和新月四种景物，赞美秋天傍晚和夜晚的美丽景色，表达对大自然的热爱。

English: The poet praises the beautiful autumn scenery and expresses his love for nature.

情感 (Emotion)

  • 热爱大自然。
  • 心情轻松、愉快、宁静。
  • 欣赏秋天的美景。

English: Love for nature, peace, joy, and appreciation of autumn beauty.

启发 (Life Lesson)

  • 学会欣赏身边的大自然。
  • 放慢脚步，发现生活中的美。
  • 观察细节，会发现平凡景物也很美丽。

English: Appreciate nature, slow down, and notice the beauty in everyday life.

写作手法 (Literary Techniques)

  • 比喻：露似真珠、月似弓。
  • 色彩描写：绿色、红色。
  • 动静结合：夕阳变化与宁静夜景。
  • 由傍晚写到夜晚，时间顺序清晰。

Memory Map

Final Exam Tips

Author: 白居易 (Bai Juyi)
Dynasty: Tang Dynasty
Theme: Beautiful autumn scenery.
Main Idea: Praise nature through vivid imagery.
Most Important Figures of Speech: Simile (比喻).

Practice Questions

  1. Who wrote the poem? 白居易
  2. What does “可怜” mean here? 可爱、令人喜爱 (lovely)
  3. What are compared in the last line? Dew→Pearls; Moon→Bow.
  4. What feeling does the poem express? Love and appreciation of nature.
  5. What is the central idea? Praising the beauty of autumn.
