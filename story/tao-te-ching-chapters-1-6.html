道
一

第一章 · 道是什么

Chapter 1 · What Is the Tao?

核心：真正的道理，没法完全说清楚；一切从"无"开始，从"有"生长。 Key idea: The true Tao cannot be fully explained in words; everything begins from "nothing" and grows into "something."
  • 能说出来的道理，就不是永恒不变的根本道理。 A truth that can be fully explained in words is not the eternal Tao.
  • 天地开始时，什么都没有；后来有了万物，万物从无到有。 In the beginning, there was nothing; later, all things came into existence from nothing.
  • 心静时，能看清本质；带着目标看，能看清边界。 When our mind is calm and free from desire, we can see the true nature of things; when we have desires, we tend to see their visible limits.
  • "无"和"有"是一体两面，都是道的一部分，深不可测，是一切的源头。 "Nothing" and "something" are two sides of the same reality; both are part of the Tao, mysterious and the source of everything.
⭐ 一句话记：道说不清，无中生有，有无一体，是万物本源。 ⭐ Remember: The Tao cannot be fully described; everything comes from nothing, and "being" and "non-being" together form the source of all things.
相
二

第二章 · 一切都是相对的

Chapter 2 · Everything Is Relative

核心：美和丑、好和坏、难和易，都是对比出来的；顺其自然，不居功，才会长久。 Key idea: Beauty and ugliness, good and bad, difficulty and ease exist through comparison; by following nature and not claiming credit, our achievements can last.
  • 知道什么是美，就知道什么是丑；知道善，就知道不善——没有绝对的好坏。 When we know what beauty is, we also know ugliness; when we know what is good, we also know what is not good.
  • 有和无、难和易、长和短、高和低，互相依存、互相成就，少了一个，另一个也不存在。 Being and non-being, difficulty and ease, long and short, high and low depend on and define each other.
  • 真正聪明的人，不瞎折腾、不强求、以身作则、少说教。 A truly wise person does not force things, but leads by example instead of constantly telling others what to do.
  • 成就万物却不占有，帮了别人却不居功；正因为不抢功劳，反而不会失去。 The wise help things grow without trying to own them and help others without claiming credit; because they do not compete for credit, their achievements remain.
⭐ 一句话记：好坏相对，顺其自然，不居功，反而长久。 ⭐ Remember: Good and bad are relative; follow nature, do not claim credit, and your achievements will last.
欲
三

第三章 · 少攀比，心就静

Chapter 3 · Less Comparison, More Peace

核心：不追捧虚名、不炫耀珍宝、不刺激欲望，人们就不会争抢、不会贪心、不会混乱。 Key idea: When people stop chasing fame, treasures, and endless desires, there will be less competition, greed, and disorder.
  • 不推崇虚名，人们就不争名夺利；不炫耀稀有物品，人们就不偷窃；不勾起贪欲，人心就不乱。 If fame is not glorified, people will compete less for status; if rare treasures are not displayed, people will be less tempted to steal; if desires are not constantly stimulated, people's minds will remain peaceful.
  • 治理/做人：净化欲望、吃饱穿暖、淡化野心、强健身心。 In governing and in life, reduce unnecessary desires, meet basic needs, weaken excessive ambition, and strengthen the body and mind.
  • 让人心保持淳朴简单，不搞阴谋诡计、不投机取巧。 Keep the mind simple and sincere instead of using tricks, schemes, or dishonest shortcuts.
  • 不妄为、不折腾、少干预，顺其自然，反而一切都能管好。 Do not interfere unnecessarily or force things; by allowing things to follow their natural course, order can emerge.
⭐ 一句话记：少虚名、少诱惑、少贪欲；简单淳朴，无为而治。 ⭐ Remember: Seek less fame, temptation, and desire; live simply and let things follow their natural course.
虚
四

第四章 · 道看不见，却永远存在

Chapter 4 · The Tao Is Invisible but Always Exists

核心：道是空的、看不见的，但永远用不完，是万物的根源。 Key idea: The Tao is empty and invisible, yet it can never be exhausted; it is the source of all things.
  • 道像一个中空的容器，永远装不满、用不尽，深邃又广大。 The Tao is like an empty container that can never be filled or used up; it is deep and limitless.
  • 收敛锋芒、化解矛盾、柔和光芒、混同尘世——低调、平和、不张扬。 Soften your sharpness, resolve conflicts, reduce your glare, and live peacefully among ordinary people — be humble, calm, and modest.
  • 道看不见、摸不着，好像不存在，却真实存在；在一切事物出现之前，它就有了。 The Tao cannot be seen or touched and may seem not to exist, yet it is always present; it existed before all things appeared.
⭐ 一句话记：道无形、用不尽、深又广；低调平和，包容万物。 ⭐ Remember: The Tao is invisible, limitless, deep, and vast; it teaches humility, peace, and acceptance.
私
五

第五章 · 天地没有偏爱

Chapter 5 · Heaven and Earth Show No Favoritism

核心：天地对万物一视同仁，没有私心；领导者也应公平公正，不偏爱、不干预。 Key idea: Heaven and Earth treat all things equally without selfish preference; a good leader should also be fair and avoid unnecessary interference.
  • 天地没有偏爱，对万物都一样；圣人也不偏爱谁，对百姓都公平。 Heaven and Earth do not favor anyone but treat all things equally; likewise, the wise should treat people fairly.
  • 天地像一个大风箱，中间是空的，越鼓动，生出的风越多——空虚反而能生生不息。 Heaven and Earth are like a bellows: empty inside, yet the more it moves, the more it produces; emptiness can create endless possibilities.
  • 话说得太多、规矩定得太细，反而容易出错、管不好；不如守住中道、简单、清净、不极端。 Too much talking and too many detailed rules can create problems; it is better to stay balanced, simple, calm, and avoid extremes.
⭐ 一句话记：天地无私，一视同仁；少言守中，清净无为。 ⭐ Remember: Heaven and Earth are impartial; be fair, speak less, stay balanced, and avoid unnecessary interference.
恒
六

第六章 · 生生不息的源头

Chapter 6 · The Endless Source of Life

核心：道是永恒的、不断创造万物的母体，看不见，却永远在孕育、永不枯竭。 Key idea: The Tao is an eternal source that continuously gives birth to all things; although invisible, it never runs dry.
  • "谷神"像山谷一样虚空，却能生养万物，永远不停、永不死亡。 The "Valley Spirit" is empty like a valley, yet it continuously gives life to all things and never dies.
  • "玄牝"是万物诞生的神秘之门，天地万物都从这里产生，是天地的根源。 The "Mysterious Mother" represents the mysterious gateway through which all things are born; it is the source of Heaven and Earth.
  • 它绵绵不绝、一直存在，看不见、摸不着，作用无穷无尽，永远用不完。 It continues endlessly; although it cannot be seen or touched, its power is unlimited and can never be exhausted.
⭐ 一句话记：道是母体，生生不息；看不见，用不尽，永恒存在。 ⭐ Remember: The Tao is the source of life; invisible and inexhaustible, it continues forever.

Chapters 1–6 · Quick Review

六章合起来一句话 · All Six Chapters in One Sentence

「道是本源，一切相对；无私无欲，顺其自然；生生不息，永恒不变。」

"The Tao is the source of all things; everything is relative, so live without selfish desire, follow the natural way, and understand that life continues endlessly."

超短背诵口诀
