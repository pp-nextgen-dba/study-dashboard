System of Equations

方程组

Two flavours of "solve them together": three linear equations sharing three unknowns, and one straight line paired with one curve. Both come down to turning many unknowns into one.

"联立求解"有两种类型：三个共享三个未知数的线性方程，以及一条直线配一条曲线。两者最终都归结为——把多个未知数化为一个。

A linear equation (dashed line) and a non-linear equation (curve) meet wherever both are satisfied at once — those meeting points are the solutions of the system.

线性方程（虚线）与非线性方程（曲线）相交的地方，正是两者同时成立之处——这些交点就是方程组的解。

3.1

Systems of Linear Equations in Three Variables

三元一次方程组

Three equations, three unknowns (x, y, z). The goal is always the same: eliminate one variable at a time until only one is left, solve it, then work backwards.

三个方程，三个未知数（x、y、z）。目标始终一致：每次消去一个未知数，直到只剩一个，解出后再逐步回代。

General Form

一般式

a₁x + b₁y + c₁z = d₁
a₂x + b₂y + c₂z = d₂
a₃x + b₃y + c₃z = d₃

Three independent equations are needed to pin down three unknowns.

三个未知数需要三个独立方程才能唯一确定。

Elimination Method

消去法

Eqn A ± Eqn B → one variable gone
方程A ± 方程B → 消去一个变量

Add or subtract pairs of equations so the same variable cancels out, leaving two equations in two unknowns.

把两个方程相加或相减，使同一个未知数互相抵消，剩下两个含两个未知数的方程。

Substitution Method

代入法

Make x (or y or z) the subject, sub into the rest
把 x（或 y、z）解出，代入其余方程

Works well when one equation already isolates a variable easily.

当某个方程已经很容易解出一个未知数时，此法尤其方便。

⚠️
Common trap: when eliminating, always check whether you need to add or subtract — matching signs (+z and +z) means subtract to cancel, opposite signs (+z and −z) means add. Getting this backwards doubles the variable instead of removing it. 常见陷阱：消元时务必先判断该加还是该减——符号相同（+z 和 +z）要相减才能抵消，符号相反（+z 和 −z）要相加才能抵消。弄反了会让变量翻倍而不是消失。
Worked Example
例题
Solve: x+y+z=6, 2x−y+z=3, x+2y−z=2
解方程组：x+y+z=6，2x−y+z=3，x+2y−z=2
(1) x+y+z=6   (2) 2x−y+z=3   (3) x+2y−z=2
(1) − (2): −x+2y = 3  ← z cancels (same sign)  → (4)
(1) + (3): 2x+3y = 8  ← z cancels (opposite sign)  → (5)
From (4): x = 2y − 3
Sub into (5): 2(2y−3) + 3y = 8 → 7y = 14 → y = 2
x = 2(2) − 3 = 1
From (1): z = 6 − x − y = 6 − 1 − 2 = 3
Answer: x=1, y=2, z=3
Memory hook: "Pick a target, kill it twice." Choose one variable to eliminate first, cancel it from two different pairs of equations, and you're left with an ordinary two-variable system you already know how to solve. 记忆口诀："锁定一个目标，消它两次。"先选定一个要消去的未知数，从两组不同的方程对中都把它消掉，剩下的就是你早已熟悉的二元方程组。

3.2

One Linear + One Non-linear Equation

一线性一非线性方程组

Here the pair is one linear equation and one non-linear equation (quadratic, circle, or similar) in two variables. The linear equation always plays the same role: it hands you one variable in terms of the other.

这里的一对方程是一个线性方程和一个非线性方程（二次、圆或类似形式），含两个变量。线性方程的作用始终不变：它能让你用一个变量表示另一个变量。

The Method

解题方法

1. Make x or y the subject of the linear eqn
1. 把 x 或 y 解出（线性方程）
2. Substitute into the non-linear eqn
2. 代入非线性方程
3. Solve the resulting equation (usually quadratic)
3. 解出所得方程（通常是二次方程）

Never rearrange the non-linear equation first — the linear one is always easier to isolate a variable from.

切勿先整理非线性方程——线性方程永远更容易解出其中一个变量。

Finding the Pairs

求出配对的解

Each x-value → substitute back for its own y
每个 x 值 → 代回求出对应的 y

A quadratic in one variable gives up to 2 values — each one must be paired with the matching value of the other variable, not mixed and matched.

一元二次方程最多给出2个值——每个值都必须与其对应的另一变量的值配对，不能任意混搭。

⚠️
Common trap: after solving for x, don't forget to substitute each x back into the linear equation to find its matching y (substituting into the non-linear equation is more error-prone and unnecessary). Also, always give solutions as (x, y) pairs, not two separate lists. 常见陷阱：求出 x 后，别忘了把每个 x 代回线性方程求出对应的 y（代回非线性方程容易出错，也没必要）。此外，答案必须以 (x, y) 配对形式给出，而不是两组各自独立的数值。
Worked Example
例题
Solve the system: x + y = 5 and x² + y² = 17
解方程组：x + y = 5 和 x² + y² = 17
From the linear eqn: y = 5 − x  ← make y the subject
Sub into x² + y² = 17:
x² + (5−x)² = 17
x² + 25 − 10x + x² = 17
2x² − 10x + 8 = 0
x² − 5x + 4 = 0  ← divide by 2
(x−1)(x−4) = 0 → x = 1 or x = 4
x=1 → y=5−1=4    x=4 → y=5−4=1
Answer: (x, y) = (1, 4) or (4, 1)
Memory hook: "Linear gives, non-linear takes." The linear equation gives you a variable in terms of the other; the non-linear equation takes that expression and turns it into a solvable one-variable equation. 记忆口诀："线性给出，非线性接收。"线性方程给出一个变量的表达式；非线性方程接收这个表达式，将其转化为可解的一元方程。
