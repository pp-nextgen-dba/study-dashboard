The role of water水的作用

Acids and alkalis show their characteristic properties only when water is present. Water allows particles to form mobile ions, so indicators can change colour and reactions can occur.

酸和碱只有在水存在时才会表现出特有性质。 水使粒子形成可移动的离子，因此指示剂能变色，化学反应也能发生。

Exam clue / 考试提示: Dry hydrogen chloride gas does not change dry blue litmus paper. Moist blue litmus paper turns red.

Daily life

Where do we use acids and alkalis?酸与碱在生活中的用途

🥤

Food & drinks食品与饮料

Carbonic acid in fizzy drinks; vinegar for pickling; tartaric/citric acids in foods.汽水中的碳酸、腌制用醋，以及食品中的酒石酸/柠檬酸。

🔋

Industry工业

Sulphuric acid in car batteries; acids and alkalis in manufacturing and cleaning.汽车电池中的硫酸；制造和清洁过程中的酸与碱。

🌱

Agriculture农业

Ammonia compounds in fertilisers; slaked lime treats acidic soil.肥料中的氨类物质；熟石灰处理酸性土壤。

💊

Health & cleaning医药与清洁

Magnesium hydroxide in antacids; potassium hydroxide in soap; sodium hydroxide in detergents.抗酸剂中的氢氧化镁、肥皂中的氢氧化钾、清洁剂中的氢氧化钠。

6.2 Neutralisation

Acid + alkali → salt + water酸 + 碱 → 盐 + 水

ACID + ALKALI → SALT + WATER
酸 + 碱 → 盐 + 水

Balanced examples 配平例子

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

H₂SO₄ + 2KOH → K₂SO₄ + 2H₂O

HNO₃ + NaOH → NaNO₃ + H₂O

Key idea: The acidic property and alkaline property are removed when the correct amounts react.

关键概念：当适量的酸与碱完全反应时，酸性和碱性会被消除。

How the salt name is formed 盐的名称怎样形成

  • Hydrochloric acid → chloride 盐酸 → 氯化物
  • Sulphuric acid → sulphate 硫酸 → 硫酸盐
  • Nitric acid → nitrate 硝酸 → 硝酸盐

🧪 Titration steps 滴定步骤

Measure the alkali.
用移液管量取一定体积的碱液，放入锥形瓶。
Add a few drops of indicator.
加入几滴指示剂（例如酚酞）。
Add acid slowly from the burette while swirling.
边摇动锥形瓶，边从滴定管慢慢加入酸。
Stop at the permanent colour change.
颜色刚好持久改变时停止。
Read the final burette value.
读取滴定管终读数。

Calculation 计算

Volume of acid used
= final burette reading − initial burette reading

Example:
= 27.60 mL − 2.40 mL
= 25.20 mL

所用酸的体积 = 滴定管终读数 − 初读数。

Applications

Neutralisation in daily life中和作用的日常应用

🦷 Teeth 牙齿

Bacteria produce acids. Mildly alkaline toothpaste helps neutralise the acids and reduce tooth decay.

细菌会产生酸。弱碱性的牙膏帮助中和酸，减少蛀牙。

🌾 Acidic soil 酸性土壤

Farmers add slaked lime to reduce soil acidity so crops can grow better.

农民加入熟石灰来降低土壤酸性，使农作物生长得更好。

👕 Fabric & hair care 衣物与护发

Acidic fabric softener can neutralise alkaline detergent residue. Slightly acidic conditioner can neutralise alkaline shampoo residue.

酸性柔顺剂可中和衣物上残留的碱性清洁剂；微酸性护发素可中和碱性洗发水残留。

🏭 Industrial waste 工业废料

Acidic waste is treated with an alkali, while alkaline waste is treated with an acid before safe discharge.

酸性废料在排放前用碱处理；碱性废料则用酸处理。

Exam technique

How to answer experiment questions实验题的高分作答法

Use the O-I-R method: Observation → Inference → Reason. 使用“观察 → 推论 → 原因”的作答结构。

1. Observation 观察
State what you can see: colour change, gas bubbles, temperature, precipitate.
2. Inference 推论
Identify acid/alkali/neutral or name the gas/substance.
3. Reason 原因
Link to the scientific concept: pH, indicator colour, neutralisation or reaction.
HOTS / KBAT

Structured questions with model answers高阶思维题与参考答案

1. Classify solutions P, Q and R from litmus observations. 根据石蕊试纸结果判断 P、Q、R。

P = acid 酸: blue litmus turns red; red litmus remains red.

Q = alkali 碱: red litmus turns blue; blue litmus remains blue.

R = neutral 中性: both litmus papers remain unchanged.

2. Magnesium ribbon is added to hydrochloric acid. What is observed and how is the gas tested? 镁带加入盐酸后有什么现象？怎样检验气体？

Gas bubbles are produced and the magnesium becomes smaller. Place a lighted wooden splint at the mouth of the test tube; a “pop” sound confirms hydrogen gas.

出现气泡，镁带逐渐变小。把点燃的木条放在试管口，若发出“啵”声，证明气体是氢气。

3. Why can vinegar help remove a fishy smell? 为什么醋可以帮助去除鱼腥味？

Fishy-smelling compounds are commonly alkaline. Vinegar contains a weak acid that neutralises the alkaline compounds, reducing the smell.

造成鱼腥味的化合物通常呈碱性。醋含弱酸，可中和碱性物质，从而减轻腥味。

4. A soil sample produces gas bubbles with baking soda solution but no bubbles with vinegar. What can be concluded? 土壤加小苏打溶液产生气泡，但加醋无气泡，可得出什么结论？

The soil is acidic. The acid in the soil reacts with baking soda and produces carbon dioxide gas. The farmer can add slaked lime to reduce the acidity.

土壤呈酸性。土壤中的酸与小苏打反应，产生二氧化碳气体。农民可加入熟石灰降低酸性。

5. In the textbook model, a sting becomes worse after soap and toothpaste are applied. What is a likely explanation? 按课本模型，叮伤处涂肥皂和牙膏后更痛，可能原因是什么？

Soap and toothpaste are alkaline, so the sting substance is likely alkaline too. An acidic substance such as vinegar would be the neutralising opposite in the simplified exam model.

肥皂和牙膏呈碱性，因此叮伤物质可能也呈碱性。按简化的考试模型，可用醋这类酸性物质来中和。

Exam note / 考试备注: This is textbook reasoning, not real-life medical first-aid guidance.

Common mistakes 常见错误

  • Writing “pH 7 is weak acid”. It is neutral.把 pH 7 写成弱酸；正确是中性。
  • For acid strength, lower pH means stronger acid.比较酸性时，pH 越低越强。
  • For alkali strength, higher pH means stronger alkali.比较碱性时，pH 越高越强。
  • Do not say “bubbles = oxygen” automatically. Acid + metal gives hydrogen.不要看到气泡就写氧气；酸与金属产生氢气。
  • Observation is not inference: “turns red” is observation; “acidic” is inference.“变红”是观察；“呈酸性”才是推论。
