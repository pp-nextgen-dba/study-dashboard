7.1 Work and Power · 功与功率

Work · 功

W = F × s

Work is done when a force causes displacement in the direction of that force.

当力使物体沿该力方向发生位移时，就做了功。

W: work (J) · F: force (N) · s: displacement (m)

Power · 功率

P = W ÷ t

Power measures how quickly work is done.

功率表示做功的快慢。

P: power (W) · W: work (J) · t: time (s)
⚠️ Important: Holding a stationary box or pushing a wall that does not move gives zero mechanical work on that object, even if you feel tired. Carrying a bag horizontally at constant height: gravity does zero work because weight acts vertically while displacement is horizontal.

Worked examples · 例题

Ali climbs stairs with a 200 N bag (p.135)
  1. Total upward force = 500 N + 200 N = 700 N.
  2. Vertical height = 5 m.
  3. W = 700 × 5 = 3,500 J.
Push a barrel with 300 N along 10 m (p.136)

W = 300 × 10 = 3,000 J (assuming the applied force acts along the displacement).

Pull a 150 N load 2 m in 40 s (p.138)

Work = 150 × 2 = 300 J. Power = 300 ÷ 40 = 7.5 W.

Raj and Yati carry 5 kg bags (p.139)

Using g = 10 m/s², bag weight = 50 N. Total weight = 500 + 50 = 550 N. Raj: P = (550 × 10)/60 = 91.7 W. Yati: P = (550 × 5)/60 = 45.8 W.

7.2 Potential & Kinetic Energy · 势能与动能

Gravitational Potential Energy · 重力势能

Eₚ = mgh

Energy due to height above a reference level. 物体因高度而具有的能量。

m (kg), g (m/s²), h (m)

Example: 30 kg lifted 1.5 m: 30 × 10 × 1.5 = 450 J.

Elastic Potential Energy · 弹性势能

Eₑ = ½Fx

Energy stored in a stretched or compressed spring, when force rises linearly from 0 to F.

弹簧被拉伸或压缩时储存的能量。

Example: 40 N compresses spring by (10 − 8) cm = 0.02 m. E = ½ × 40 × 0.02 = 0.4 J.

Kinetic Energy · 动能

Eₖ = ½mv²

Energy of motion. 运动中的物体具有的能量。

m (kg), v (m/s)

Example: 2,000 kg car at 18 km/h = 5 m/s. E = ½ × 2,000 × 5² = 25,000 J.

Why is elastic energy ½Fx?

The force increases as the spring stretches. On a straight-line force–extension graph, energy equals the triangular area beneath the line.

At 40 cm extension, force = 8 N: E = ½ × 8 × 0.40 = 1.6 J.

📌 Unit conversions: 1 kg = 1,000 g; 1 m = 100 cm; km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s. For these textbook questions, use g = 10 m/s² unless otherwise stated.

7.3 Conservation of Energy · 能量守恒定律

Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it changes from one form into another. 能量不能凭空产生或消失，只能从一种形式转化为另一种形式。

Total energy before = Total energy after

🍎 Falling apple

Gravitational potential → kinetic

重力势能 → 动能

🪭 Electric fan

Electrical → kinetic (+ heat and sound)

电能 → 动能（也产生热和声音）

🌱 Photosynthesis

Light → chemical energy

光能 → 化学能

🚗 Car

Chemical → kinetic (+ heat and sound)

化学能 → 动能（也产生热和声音）

Loaded spring · 弹簧振子

At the turning points, speed is zero and elastic potential energy is highest relative to equilibrium. At equilibrium, speed and kinetic energy are highest in the idealised model.

🔎 Physics precision: A closed system can exchange energy but not matter with its surroundings; an isolated system exchanges neither. Mechanical energy stays constant when no non-conservative work is done. With friction, some mechanical energy becomes thermal energy, but total energy is still conserved.

Worked energy-conversion examples

Spring launches a 50 g ball (p.151)
  1. Elastic energy = ½ × 10 N × 0.05 m = 0.25 J.
  2. Mass = 50 g = 0.05 kg.
  3. ½mv² = 0.25 → v² = (2 × 0.25)/0.05 = 10.
  4. v = √10 ≈ 3.2 m/s.
Trampoline textbook example (p.152)

Using the textbook's model: total force = (40 × 10) + 100 = 500 N; elastic energy = ½ × 500 × 0.05 = 12.5 J. Set mgh = 12.5 J: h = 12.5/(40 × 10) = 0.03125 m ≈ 3.1 cm.

This reproduces the textbook's simplified calculation; actual trampoline motion depends on spring force, body movement and the chosen reference height.
