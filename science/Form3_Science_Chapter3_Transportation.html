3.1 Transport System in Organisms 生物体内的运输系统

Simple organisms (unicellular) 单细胞生物No specialised transport system. Substances enter and leave cells by diffusion through the cell membrane.
没有专门的运输系统；物质通过细胞膜扩散。
Complex organisms (multicellular) 多细胞生物Need specialised transport because diffusion alone is too slow for distant cells.
细胞距离较远，单靠扩散速度太慢，因此需要运输系统。
Humans & animals 人类与动物Blood circulatory system transports oxygen, nutrients and wastes.
血液循环系统运输氧气、养分及废物。
Plants 植物Xylem transports water/minerals; phloem transports sugars.
木质部运输水和矿物盐；韧皮部运输糖分。

Human heart structure 人类心脏结构

Simplified functional schematic (not anatomical scale). Blue = deoxygenated blood; red = oxygenated blood. 蓝色：缺氧血；红色：含氧血。

Atrium 心房Receives blood; thin muscular wall. 接收血液，肌肉壁较薄。
Ventricle 心室Pumps blood out; thick wall. Left ventricle thickest. 将血液泵出；左心室壁最厚。
Valves 瓣膜Prevent backflow. Tricuspid on right; bicuspid/mitral on left. 防止血液倒流。
Septum 中隔Separates left and right sides; prevents mixing. 分隔左右两侧，防止血液混合。

Blood flow sequence 血液流动顺序

Body 身体→Vena cava 腔静脉→Right atrium 右心房→Right ventricle 右心室→Pulmonary artery 肺动脉→Lungs 肺→Pulmonary veins 肺静脉→Left atrium 左心房→Left ventricle 左心室→Aorta 主动脉→Body 身体
Memory trick 记忆法: RIGHT → LUNGS; LEFT → BODY. 右心送血到肺，左心送血到全身。Pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood; pulmonary veins carry oxygenated blood.

Blood pressure and pulse 血压与脉搏

120/75 mmHg: 120 = systolic pressure 收缩压; 75 = diastolic pressure 舒张压. Blood pressure is measured with a sphygmomanometer (血压计).

Pulse rate 脉搏率: Influenced by physical activity 运动、age 年龄、sex 性别 and health 身体状况. These are general trends, not fixed rules for every person. 这些只是一般趋势，不适用于每个人。

3.3 Human Blood 人类血液

Blood carries oxygen, nutrients, hormones and wastes. 血液运输氧气、养分、激素和废物。

Plasma 血浆 ≈55%About 90% water; carries dissolved substances. 约90%为水，运输溶解物质。
Red blood cells 红血球 ≈45%Carry oxygen using haemoglobin. 通过血红蛋白运输氧气。
White cells + platelets 白血球和血小板 <1%White cells defend against infection; platelets help blood clot. 白血球抵抗感染；血小板帮助凝血。

Blood components can be separated by centrifugation (离心法). Percentages are approximate.

ABO blood groups ABO血型

Blood group 血型Antigen on RBC 红血球抗原Antibodies in plasma 血浆抗体Can receive RBC from 可接受红血球
AAAnti-B 抗BA, O
BBAnti-A 抗AB, O
ABA and BNeither anti-A nor anti-B 无A, B, AB, O
ONeither A nor B 无Anti-A and anti-B 抗A与抗BO
Memory trick 记忆法: Group O is the universal ABO red-cell donor; group AB is the universal ABO red-cell recipient. Real transfusions must also consider Rh factor and crossmatching; the table is only for ABO red-cell compatibility. O型是ABO红血球通用捐献者；AB型是ABO红血球通用接受者，但实际输血还须考虑Rh及配血试验。
Why incompatible blood is dangerous? 为什么不相容输血危险？

Recipient antibodies bind to matching antigens on donor red cells, causing agglutination (clumping) and possibly haemolysis. 接受者的抗体与捐献者红血球上的相应抗原结合，引起凝集，甚至溶血。

3.4 Transport System in Plants 植物运输系统

Transpiration 蒸腾作用

Loss of water as water vapour mainly through stomata on leaves. 植物主要通过叶片气孔，以水蒸气形式失水。

Roots absorb water 根吸水→Xylem carries water 木质部向上运输→Evaporation from leaves 叶片蒸发→Transpiration pull 蒸腾拉力

Transpiration vs guttation 蒸腾作用与吐水作用

Transpiration 蒸腾Guttation / exudation 吐水
Water vapour 水蒸气Liquid droplets 液态水滴
Mostly stomata 主要经气孔Hydathodes 水孔
Often stronger in daylight 通常白天较强Often at night / high humidity 常在夜间或高湿度时
Not dew! 不是露珠！ Dew is condensed water vapour from the atmosphere; guttation comes from water pushed out of the plant. 露珠来自空气水汽凝结；吐水来自植物体内排出的水。

Factors affecting transpiration 影响蒸腾速率的因素

Temperature 温度 ↑Transpiration generally ↑
通常蒸腾 ↑
Light intensity 光照 ↑Stomata often open; transpiration ↑
气孔通常开放，蒸腾 ↑
Wind speed 风速 ↑Transpiration ↑
蒸腾 ↑
Air humidity 湿度 ↑Transpiration ↓
蒸腾 ↓
Number of stomata 气孔数量 ↑Potential transpiration ↑
潜在蒸腾 ↑

These are general trends when other conditions remain similar; very hot or dry conditions may close stomata and reduce water loss. 其他条件相同时的一般趋势；极热或干旱可能导致气孔关闭。

Potometers 蒸腾计

Mass potometer 质量式蒸腾计Estimates water loss by change in mass per unit time.
速率 ≈ 质量变化 ÷ 时间
Bubble potometer 气泡式蒸腾计Measures water uptake using air-bubble movement in a capillary tube. 通过气泡移动估算吸水速率。
Bubble speed = distance ÷ time; to find volume uptake rate, multiply by tube cross-sectional area.

Vascular bundles 维管束

Xylem 木质部Phloem 韧皮部
Transports water and mineral salts 运输水与矿物盐Transports sucrose and other organic substances 运输蔗糖及有机物
Mostly upward: roots → leaves 主要向上：根→叶Source → sink; can be up or down 从来源到消耗／储存部位，可向上或向下
Transport mainly in dead conducting cells 主要由死的导管细胞运输Transport through living sieve tubes 经活筛管运输
Ring-barking experiment 环剥实验: Removing a ring of bark interrupts phloem. Sugars accumulate above the cut, causing swelling. 去除一圈树皮会切断韧皮部，糖分在切口上方累积，形成肿胀。
