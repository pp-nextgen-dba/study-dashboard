🌍 10.1 Development in Astronomy 天文学的发展

Astronomical models changed as observations and scientific evidence improved. 随着观测技术与科学证据进步，人类对太阳系的认识不断改变。

1. Geocentric Model

Claudius Ptolemy • Earth at the centre; Sun and planets orbit Earth in circles; Earth considered stationary.

地心说：托勒密认为地球位于中心、保持静止；太阳与行星绕地球作圆周运动。

2. Heliocentric Model

Nicolaus Copernicus • Sun at the centre; Earth and other planets orbit the Sun in circles; Earth rotates on its axis.

日心说：哥白尼认为太阳位于中心，行星绕太阳作圆周运动，地球也会自转。

3. Kepler's Modified Model

Johannes Kepler • Planets move in elliptical orbits; the Sun is at one focus, not the centre of each ellipse.

开普勒修正日心说：行星沿椭圆轨道运行；太阳位于椭圆的一个焦点，而不是椭圆中心。

Exam trap: Copernicus used circular orbits; Kepler corrected them to ellipses. The Sun is at a focus, not the geometric centre. 考试重点：哥白尼＝圆形轨道；开普勒＝椭圆轨道；太阳位于焦点。

🕰 10.2 Space Exploration Timeline 太空探索发展时间线

11th century — Early rockets used in China following the invention of gunpowder. 中国使用早期火箭。
1609 — Galileo used an improved telescope for astronomical observations. 伽利略改进并使用望远镜进行天文观测。
1957 — Sputnik 1: first artificial satellite, launched by USSR. 第一颗人造卫星。
1961 — Yuri Gagarin: first human to orbit Earth, aboard Vostok 1. 首位绕地球飞行的人类。
1969 — Neil Armstrong: first person to step on the Moon, Apollo 11. 首位踏上月球的人。
1973 — Pioneer 10: first spacecraft to fly past Jupiter. 首次飞掠木星。
1981 — Columbia: first orbital flight of the US Space Shuttle programme. 哥伦比亚号航天飞机首次轨道飞行。
1989 — Voyager 2: first spacecraft to fly past Neptune. 首次飞掠海王星。
1990 — Hubble Space Telescope launched aboard Discovery. 哈勃太空望远镜发射。
1996 — Malaysian MEASAT-1 and MEASAT-2 launched. 马来西亚通信卫星发射。
2000 — TiungSAT-1, Malaysia's first microsatellite, launched. 马来西亚首颗微型卫星。
2002 — Malaysian National Space Agency (ANGKASA) established. 国家航天局成立。
2011 — Main assembly of the International Space Station completed. 国际空间站主体组装完成。
Scientific accuracy: Galileo did not invent the first telescope; he improved an existing design for astronomy. The textbook's 2011 ISS date refers to its principal assembly, not the end of all upgrades. 补充：伽利略并非望远镜的最初发明者；2011年是空间站主要组装阶段完成。

🛰 Space Exploration Technologies 太空探索技术

🔭 Space Telescope 太空望远镜

Observes space from above Earth's atmosphere, avoiding atmospheric blurring and absorption of some wavelengths.

Examples: Hubble, James Webb.

在大气层外观测宇宙，减少大气干扰，获得更清晰或不同波段的观测数据。

🚀 Rocket 火箭

Launches satellites, spacecraft and probes. Exhaust gases expelled backwards generate forward thrust (Newton's third law).

发射卫星与航天器。高速喷出的气体产生反方向推力（牛顿第三定律）。

🛰 Satellite 人造卫星

Orbits a celestial body, commonly Earth. Used for telecommunications, navigation, weather, remote sensing and defence.

围绕天体运行，用于通信、导航、气象、遥感及国防。

🪐 Space Probe 太空探测器

Uncrewed spacecraft that explores space, collects measurements and transmits data to Earth. May fly past, orbit or land on targets.

无人航天器，探测遥远天体，收集资料并传回地球。

Remember: A rocket provides launch thrust; a satellite stays in orbit; a probe explores; a space telescope observes astronomical objects. 记忆：火箭负责发射；卫星负责绕行；探测器负责探索；太空望远镜负责观测。

📡 Remote Sensing 遥感技术

Definition: A method of collecting and recording information about an object or area from a distance, without direct contact. 定义：无需直接接触，从远距离收集与记录目标或地区的信息。

🌱 Agriculture 农业

Identify suitable agricultural areas; monitor crops and land conditions.

寻找适合农业开发的地区，监测农作物与土地状况。

🪨 Geology 地质学

Locate mineral resources and monitor land changes or degradation.

探测矿产资源位置、土地变化与退化。

🔥 Disaster Management 灾害管理

Detect and monitor floods, forest fires, pollution and other natural hazards.

监测洪水、森林火灾、污染及其他灾害。

🛡 Defence 国防

Monitor borders and detect potentially intruding ships, aircraft and vehicles.

监控边境，探测可能入侵的船只、飞机与车辆。

Exam response: “Remote sensing enables information about Earth's surface to be obtained from a distance using sensors on satellites or aircraft.” 考试答题：遥感技术利用卫星或飞机上的感应器，从远处取得地表信息。

✍️ Exam-Ready Short Answers 考试标准简答

Q: State two differences between geocentric and heliocentric models.

1. Geocentric: Earth is at the centre; heliocentric: Sun is at the centre.
2. Geocentric: Earth is stationary; heliocentric: Earth rotates on its axis and revolves around the Sun.

地心说以地球为中心并认为地球静止；日心说以太阳为中心，地球自转并公转。

Q: Why is a space telescope useful?

It operates above Earth's atmosphere, reducing atmospheric interference and allowing clearer observations of celestial objects.

因为太空望远镜位于大气层外，减少大气干扰，更清晰地观测天体。

Q: Explain how a rocket moves upwards.

Hot exhaust gases are expelled downwards at high speed, producing an upward reaction force that propels the rocket.

高温气体高速向下喷出，产生向上的反作用力，推动火箭升空。

Q: Give two applications of remote sensing.

It can identify suitable land for agriculture and detect the locations of forest fires for disaster management.

可用于寻找适合耕种的土地，以及侦测森林火灾位置。
