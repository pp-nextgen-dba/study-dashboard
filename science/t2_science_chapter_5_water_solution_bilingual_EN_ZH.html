🌤️ 1. Evaporation of Water 水｜水的蒸发

Evaporation 蒸发 happens when water molecules at the surface gain enough energy to escape into the air as water vapour.

💨 Humidity｜湿度

Lower humidity → faster evaporation.
湿度越低 → 蒸发越快。

Dry air contains less water vapour, so it can receive more water molecules.
干燥空气含较少水蒸气，因此可容纳更多蒸发出来的水分子。

🌡️ Temperature｜温度

Higher temperature → faster evaporation.
温度越高 → 蒸发越快。

Water 水 molecules gain kinetic energy, move faster and escape more easily.
水分子获得更多动能，运动更快，更容易离开液面。

📐 Surface Area｜表面积

Larger exposed surface → faster evaporation.
暴露表面积越大 → 蒸发越快。

More surface molecules can escape at the same time.
同一时间有更多表面水分子可以逸出。

🌬️ Movement of Air｜空气流动

Faster air movement → faster evaporation.
空气流动越快 → 蒸发越快。

Moving air carries water vapour away from the surface.
流动的空气把液面附近的水蒸气带走。

Memory trick 记忆口诀: H-T-S-A → Humidity 湿度 · Temperature 温度 · Surface area 表面积 · Air movement 空气流动。

Daily-life applications｜日常生活应用

👕 Clothes are spread out to increase exposed surface area.
衣服摊开可增加表面积，加快蒸发。
💇 A hair dryer supplies warm moving air.
吹风机提供温暖且流动的空气，加快蒸发。
🧂 Sea salt can be obtained by evaporating seawater.
蒸发海水可获得海盐。
🐟 Drying food removes water, helping it keep longer.
晒干食物可除去水分，延长保存时间。
Exam 考点：Why do we feel cold when we sweat? 为什么流汗时会觉得凉？
Sweat evaporates and absorbs heat from the body, producing a cooling effect.
汗液蒸发时从身体吸收热量，因此产生降温效果。

🥤 2. Solute, Solvent & Solution｜溶质、溶剂与溶液

Term 术语Meaning 含义Example 例子：糖水
Solute 溶质Substance dissolved in a solvent
溶解在溶剂中的物质		Sugar 糖
Solvent 溶剂Liquid that dissolves a solute
能溶解溶质的液体		Water 水
Solution 溶液Uniform mixture formed after dissolving
溶质溶于溶剂后形成的均匀混合物		Sugar 糖 water

Concentration｜浓度

Dilute｜稀溶液

Small amount of solute; more can still dissolve.
溶质较少，还能继续溶解更多溶质。

Concentrated｜浓溶液

More solute is present.
含有较多溶质，可继续溶解的量较少。

Saturated｜饱和溶液

Cannot dissolve more solute under the same conditions.
在相同条件下不能再溶解更多溶质，多余溶质保持未溶解状态。

Solubility 溶解度 = maximum amount of solute that can dissolve in a given amount of solvent at a specific temperature.
中文：在特定温度下，一定量溶剂所能溶解的溶质最大量。

⚡ 3. Factors Affecting Dissolving Rate｜影响溶解速率的因素

🌡️ Temperature｜温度

Higher solvent temperature → solute dissolves faster.

🥄 Stirring｜搅拌

Faster stirring → faster dissolving.
搅拌越快 → 溶解越快，因为溶剂与溶质接触更充分。

🔹 Solute Size｜溶质颗粒大小

Smaller particles → larger total surface area → faster dissolving.
颗粒越小 → 总表面积越大 → 溶解越快。

Remember 记忆： Hot 热 + Stir 搅拌 + Small 小颗粒 = Faster dissolving 更快溶解。
Important distinction: rate of solubility/dissolving describes how quickly a solute dissolves. Solubility describes how much can dissolve under specified conditions.

🌍 5. Water 水 & Organic Solvents

Water 水 as a universal solvent

Water 水 dissolves many substances and is widely used as a solvent in homes, agriculture, medicine and industry.

Examples: fertilisers dissolved for plant uptake, soft drinks, and detergents used in cleaning.

🚰 6. Water 水 Purification Methods

Filtration 过滤

Separates suspended particles from liquid.
把悬浮颗粒与水分开。

Boiling

Kills microorganisms.
杀死微生物。

Chlorination 氯化

Chlorine kills microorganisms.
氯可杀死微生物。

Distillation｜蒸馏

Separates water from suspended and dissolved impurities.
把水与悬浮物及溶解物质分离，可得到更纯净的水。

Which method produces pure water? Distillation｜蒸馏. Water 水 is vaporised and then condensed, leaving non-volatile dissolved and suspended impurities behind.

🧪 Exam Experiments｜考试必记实验

Experiment A｜Temperature 温度

Aim 目的：Study the effect of solvent temperature on dissolving rate.
研究溶剂温度对溶解速率的影响。

Manipulated variable 操纵变量：Temperature of water 水温
Responding variable 应变量：Time/rate of dissolving 溶解时间／速率
Constant variables 固定变量：Amount of solute, volume of water, stirring method 溶质量、水量、搅拌方式

Conclusion 结论：Higher temperature → faster dissolving.
温度越高 → 溶解越快。

Experiment B｜Stirring 搅拌

Aim 目的：Study the effect of stirring on dissolving rate.
研究搅拌对溶解速率的影响。

Manipulated variable 操纵变量：Stirring rate 搅拌速度
Responding variable 应变量：Time/rate of dissolving 溶解时间／速率
Constant variables 固定变量：Temperature, amount/size of solute, volume of water 温度、溶质量与颗粒大小、水量

Conclusion 结论：Faster stirring → faster dissolving.
搅拌越快 → 溶解越快。

Experiment C｜Particle size 颗粒大小

Aim 目的：Study the effect of solute particle size.
研究溶质颗粒大小对溶解速率的影响。

Manipulated variable 操纵变量：Particle size 颗粒大小
Responding variable 应变量：Time/rate of dissolving 溶解时间／速率
Constant variables 固定变量：Temperature, amount of solute, water volume 温度、溶质量、水量

Conclusion 结论：Smaller particles → larger surface area → faster dissolving.
颗粒越小 → 总表面积越大 → 溶解越快。

🎯 9. Quick Exam Revision｜快速考试复习

1. What four factors affect evaporation?

Humidity, surrounding temperature, exposed surface area and movement of air.

2. Why does a hair dryer dry hair faster?

It increases temperature and air movement, increasing the rate of evaporation.

3. What is the difference between a solute and solvent?

The solute is dissolved; the solvent is the liquid that dissolves the solute.

4. What happens when a solution becomes saturated?

It cannot dissolve more solute under those conditions; extra solute remains undissolved.

5. Name three factors that increase the rate of dissolving.

Higher temperature, faster stirring and smaller solute particle size.

6. How can you distinguish a suspension from a solution?

A suspension is cloudy, its particles may settle and filtration leaves residue. A true solution is uniform and does not leave dissolved solute on ordinary filter paper.

7. What is alum used for in water treatment?

It causes fine suspended/mud particles to clump together, helping them settle.

8. What does slaked lime do?

It reduces the acidity of water.

9. Why is chlorine added?

To kill microorganisms.

10. Which purification method can produce pure water?

Distillation｜蒸馏.

⭐ Final 60-second memory map｜60秒终极记忆图

EVAPORATION 蒸发: humidity ↓, temperature ↑, surface area ↑, air movement ↑ → evaporation faster.

DISSOLVING RATE 溶解速率: temperature ↑ + stirring ↑ + particle size ↓ → dissolves faster.

MIXTURES 混合物: solution = dissolved & uniform; suspension = undissolved particles; colloid = intermediate dispersed mixture.

PURIFICATION 净化: filtration removes particles; boiling/chlorine kill microorganisms; distillation gives the most complete separation.

WATER TREATMENT 水处理: filter → oxidise → coagulate → sediment → filter → disinfect.
