🌤️ 1. Evaporation of Water

Evaporation happens when water molecules at the surface gain enough energy to escape into the air as water vapour.

💨 Humidity

Lower humidity → faster evaporation.

Dry air contains less water vapour, so it can receive more water molecules.

🌡️ Temperature

Higher temperature → faster evaporation.

Water molecules gain kinetic energy, move faster and escape more easily.

📐 Surface Area

Larger exposed surface → faster evaporation.

More surface molecules can escape at the same time.

🌬️ Movement of Air

Faster air movement → faster evaporation.

Moving air carries water vapour away from the surface.

Memory trick: H-T-S-A → Humidity, Temperature, Surface area, Air movement.

Daily-life applications

👕 Clothes are spread out to increase exposed surface area.
💇 A hair dryer supplies warm moving air.
🧂 Sea salt can be obtained by evaporating seawater.
🐟 Drying food removes water, helping it keep longer.
Why do we feel cold when we sweat? Sweat evaporates from the skin. Higher-energy water molecules escape and heat is taken from the body, producing a cooling effect.

🥤 2. Solute, Solvent & Solution

TermMeaningExample: sugar water
SoluteSubstance that dissolves in a solventSugar
SolventLiquid that dissolves a substanceWater
SolutionUniform mixture formed when solute dissolves in solventSugar water

Concentration

Dilute

Small amount of solute. It can still dissolve more solute.

Concentrated

More solute is present. It can dissolve less additional solute.

Saturated

Cannot dissolve any more solute at that condition. Excess solute remains undissolved.

Solubility = the maximum amount of solute that can dissolve in a given amount of solvent at a specific temperature.

⚡ 3. Factors Affecting Rate of Solubility

🌡️ Temperature

Higher solvent temperature → solute dissolves faster.

🥄 Stirring

Faster stirring → faster dissolving because fresh solvent contacts the solute more rapidly.

🔹 Solute Size

Smaller particles → larger total exposed surface area → faster dissolving.

Remember: Hot + Stir + Small = Faster dissolving.
Important distinction: rate of solubility/dissolving describes how quickly a solute dissolves. Solubility describes how much can dissolve under specified conditions.

🌍 5. Water & Organic Solvents

Water as a universal solvent

Water dissolves many substances and is widely used as a solvent in homes, agriculture, medicine and industry.

Examples: fertilisers dissolved for plant uptake, soft drinks, and detergents used in cleaning.

Organic solvents

SolventExample use

KeroseneLamp oil

Some organic solvents are volatile and potentially harmful; they should be handled appropriately.

🚰 6. Water Purification Methods

Filtration

Separates suspended particles from liquid.

Boiling

Kills microorganisms.

Chlorination

Chlorine kills microorganisms.

Distillation

Separates water from suspended particles and dissolved substances; microorganisms are also eliminated in the process.

Which method produces pure water? Distillation. Water is vaporised and then condensed, leaving non-volatile dissolved and suspended impurities behind.

🏭 7. Water Treatment & Supply System

Learn this sequence carefully:

1️⃣ Initial filtration→2️⃣ Oxidation→3️⃣ Coagulation→4️⃣ Sedimentation→5️⃣ Filtration→6️⃣ Chlorination & fluoridation
StageFunction
Initial filtrationRemoves large suspended material such as leaves and branches.
OxidationIncreases oxygen content and helps remove unpleasant smell and taste.
CoagulationAlum helps fine mud particles clump together; slaked lime (calcium hydroxide) reduces acidity.
SedimentationSuspended particles settle at the bottom.
FiltrationSand filters remove remaining suspended particles.
ChlorinationChlorine kills microorganisms.
FluoridationFluoride compound is added to help prevent tooth decay.
Think like a cleaning pipeline: remove big material → improve water → clump dirt → let dirt settle → filter fine dirt → disinfect.

🌱 8. Water Sustainability & Pollution

🏠 Domestic waste

Improve sewerage and sanitation, manage rubbish correctly and educate communities.

🏭 Industrial waste

Treat industrial effluent before discharge and enforce environmental regulations.

🌾 Agricultural chemicals

Encourage responsible use of fertilisers and pesticides, including biodegradable alternatives where appropriate.

🛢️ Oil spills

Contain spills quickly and improve monitoring and response.

Alternative water-supply ideas: water recycling, fog harvesting and desalination of seawater using reverse osmosis.

🎯 9. Quick Exam Revision

1. What four factors affect evaporation?

Humidity, surrounding temperature, exposed surface area and movement of air.

2. Why does a hair dryer dry hair faster?

It increases temperature and air movement, increasing the rate of evaporation.

3. What is the difference between a solute and solvent?

The solute is dissolved; the solvent is the liquid that dissolves the solute.

4. What happens when a solution becomes saturated?

It cannot dissolve more solute under those conditions; extra solute remains undissolved.

5. Name three factors that increase the rate of dissolving.

Higher temperature, faster stirring and smaller solute particle size.

6. How can you distinguish a suspension from a solution?

A suspension is cloudy, its particles may settle and filtration leaves residue. A true solution is uniform and does not leave dissolved solute on ordinary filter paper.

7. What is alum used for in water treatment?

It causes fine suspended/mud particles to clump together, helping them settle.

8. What does slaked lime do?

It reduces the acidity of water.

9. Why is chlorine added?

To kill microorganisms.

10. Which purification method can produce pure water?

Distillation.

⭐ Final 60-second memory map

EVAPORATION: humidity ↓, temperature ↑, surface area ↑, air movement ↑ → evaporation faster.

DISSOLVING RATE: temperature ↑ + stirring ↑ + particle size ↓ → dissolves faster.

MIXTURES: solution = dissolved & uniform; suspension = undissolved particles; colloid = intermediate dispersed mixture.

PURIFICATION: filtration removes particles; boiling/chlorine kill microorganisms; distillation gives the most complete separation.

WATER TREATMENT: filter → oxidise → coagulate → sediment → filter → disinfect.
