01 · The big idea / 核心概念

Thermochemistry studies heat changes associated with chemical reactions. The key question is: Does the process absorb heat from, or release heat to, the surroundings?

热化学研究化学反应中的热量变化。最重要的问题是：过程从周围环境吸收热量，还是向周围环境释放热量？

Endothermic · 吸热

Heat moves from surroundings into the system. The surroundings usually cool down.

热量从环境进入体系，周围环境通常变冷。

Surroundings → System

ΔH > 0 (for a chemical reaction)

Exothermic · 放热

Heat moves from the system to surroundings. The surroundings usually warm up.

热量从体系释放到环境，周围环境通常变热。

System → Surroundings

ΔH < 0 (for a chemical reaction)

Memory trick: ENDO = heat ENters; EXO = heat EXits. A cold cup usually signals endothermic; a warm cup usually signals exothermic, assuming similar conditions.记忆法：ENDO = 热量进入；EXO = 热量离开。其他条件相近时，杯子变冷通常表示吸热，变热通常表示放热。

Important distinction: Melting, evaporation and condensation are physical changes, not chemical reactions. They still involve heat absorption or release. Photosynthesis uses light energy rather than simply absorbing heat from the Sun.

重要区别：熔化、蒸发和凝结属于物理变化，不是化学反应，但同样涉及吸热或放热。光合作用主要利用光能，并非单纯吸收太阳热量。

03 · Experiments / 实验

Experiment A · 吸热实验

Dissolve ammonium chloride in water in a polystyrene cup. Measure the initial and final temperatures with a thermometer.

在聚苯乙烯杯中把氯化铵溶于水，用温度计记录反应前后的温度。

NH₄Cl + water→Temperature ↓

Observation: Final temperature is lower. Inference: Heat is absorbed from the surroundings.

观察：最终温度较低。推论：体系从周围环境吸热。

Experiment B · 放热实验

Mix sodium hydroxide solution with dilute sulfuric acid in a polystyrene cup and record the temperature change.

在聚苯乙烯杯中混合氢氧化钠溶液和稀硫酸，记录温度变化。

Acid + alkali→Temperature ↑

Observation: Final temperature is higher. Inference: Heat is released to the surroundings.

观察：最终温度较高。推论：反应向周围环境放热。

2NaOH + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O

Exam skill: Manipulated variable: substances mixed. Responding variable: temperature change. Controlled variables may include initial temperature and solution volume. Polystyrene reduces heat exchange with the environment.

考试技巧：操纵性变量：混合的物质。反应性变量：温度变化。控制变量可包括初始温度和溶液体积。聚苯乙烯杯可减少与外界的热量交换。

04 · Daily-life examples / 生活实例

Absorbs heat / 吸热

🧊 Ice melting / 冰融化 — heat breaks the solid structure.

💨 Evaporation / 蒸发 — water absorbs energy to become vapour.

🍞 Baking bread / 烤面包 — bread absorbs oven heat; several physical and chemical changes occur.

🧊 Instant cold pack / 即用冷敷袋 — ammonium chloride dissolving can absorb heat.

Releases heat / 放热

🔥 Burning coal / 煤燃烧 — combustion releases energy.

🧪 Neutralisation / 中和反应 — acid and alkali typically release heat.

🔩 Rusting / 生锈 — slow oxidation releases heat, often too slowly to feel.

💧 Condensation / 凝结 — water vapour releases latent heat as it becomes liquid.

Scientific caution / 科学补充

Not every detergent produces a warming effect when dissolved; it depends on its formulation. The textbook's bee-sting baking-soda advice should not be used as first-aid guidance: remove the stinger if present, wash the area and use a cold compress; seek urgent care for breathing difficulty or signs of severe allergy. Do not attempt chemical neutralisation on skin.

并非所有洗衣粉溶解都会升温，效果取决于配方。课本关于蜂蜇后涂小苏打的说法不应作为急救建议：若有残留毒刺应尽快去除、清洗并冷敷；如呼吸困难或出现严重过敏症状应立即就医。不要在皮肤上尝试酸碱中和。

05 · Exam answer framework / 考试答题模板

Question: Why does the temperature decrease when ammonium chloride dissolves in water?

题目：为什么氯化铵溶于水时温度下降？

Model answer: Dissolving ammonium chloride is an endothermic process. Heat energy is absorbed from the water and surroundings, causing the measured temperature to decrease.

参考答案：氯化铵溶解属于吸热过程。它从水和周围环境吸收热量，因此测得的温度下降。

Question: State one measured parameter used to distinguish the two types of process.

题目：写出一个可以区分两类过程的测量参数。

Answer / 答案: Change in temperature (°C) / 温度变化（°C）。

07 · 60-second revision / 一分钟复习

1. Endothermic absorbs heat; surroundings cool. 2. Exothermic releases heat; surroundings warm. 3. Measure temperature before and after. 4. Ammonium chloride dissolving is endothermic; acid–alkali neutralisation is exothermic. 5. Melting and evaporation absorb heat; condensation releases heat. 6. Physical changes are not chemical reactions.

1. 吸热过程吸收热量，环境变冷。2. 放热过程释放热量，环境变热。3. 测量过程前后的温度。4. 氯化铵溶解吸热；酸碱中和放热。5. 熔化和蒸发吸热；凝结放热。6. 物理变化不等于化学反应。
