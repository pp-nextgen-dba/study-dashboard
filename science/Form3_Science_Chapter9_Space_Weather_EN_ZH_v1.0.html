Core idea / 核心概念: The Sun produces energy and sometimes releases energetic radiation and charged particles. Earth's magnetic environment deflects many charged particles, but strong solar activity can still affect technology. 太阳产生能量，也会释放高能辐射及带电粒子。地球磁场能偏转许多带电粒子，但强烈的太阳活动仍可能影响科技系统。

01 · Structure of the Sun · 太阳的结构

The Sun is a star made mainly of hydrogen and helium. Energy is generated by nuclear fusion in the core. 太阳是一颗主要由氢和氦组成的恒星，核心通过核聚变释放能量。

Core · 核心

Hydrogen nuclei fuse to form helium, releasing energy.

氢核聚变形成氦，释放大量能量。

Radiation zone · 辐射层

Energy moves outward mainly by radiation.

能量主要以辐射方式向外传递。

Convection zone · 对流层

Hot plasma rises; cooler plasma sinks.

热等离子体上升，较冷的等离子体下沉。

Photosphere · 光球层

Visible “surface” of the Sun; sunspots and granules are seen here.

太阳可见的“表面”，可观察到太阳黑子与米粒组织。

Chromosphere · 色球层

Reddish layer visible during a total solar eclipse.

日全食期间可观察到的偏红色大气层。

Corona · 日冕

Very hot, extended outer atmosphere, best seen during a total solar eclipse.

非常炽热、延伸很远的最外层大气，在日全食时较容易看见。

📝 Memorise the order (centre → outside): Core → Radiation zone → Convection zone → Photosphere → Chromosphere → Corona.
由内向外：核心 → 辐射层 → 对流层 → 光球层 → 色球层 → 日冕。

02 · Solar surface phenomena · 太阳活动现象

Granules · 米粒组织

Grain-like patterns in the photosphere caused by convection; typically ~1,000 km across.

光球层上的颗粒状结构，由对流形成，直径通常约 1,000 km。

Sunspots · 太阳黑子

Dark-looking, cooler regions with intense magnetic fields. Their numbers vary in an approximately 11-year solar cycle.

温度比周围低、磁场强的暗区；黑子数量约每 11 年呈现一个周期。

Prominence · 日珥

A large, glowing loop or arch of plasma held by magnetic fields; can last days or longer.

受磁场约束的巨大弧形发光等离子体，可持续数天甚至更久。

Solar flare · 太阳耀斑

A sudden burst of electromagnetic radiation from a magnetically active region. Its light reaches Earth in about 8 minutes.

太阳活跃区域突然释放的强烈电磁辐射；光约 8 分钟抵达地球。

Coronal mass ejection (CME) · 日冕物质抛射

A huge cloud of magnetised plasma expelled from the Sun. It may reach Earth in roughly 1–3 days, sometimes faster or slower.

太阳向太空抛出的大量带磁场等离子体，通常约 1–3 天到达地球，实际时间会变化。

Solar wind · 太阳风

A continuous stream of charged particles flowing outward from the Sun, carrying the interplanetary magnetic field.

太阳持续向外释放的带电粒子流，同时携带行星际磁场。

⚠️ Important scientific distinction / 课本延伸纠正

A solar flare is primarily a radiation burst; a CME ejects matter. Both may occur together, but they are not identical. Auroras are mainly caused by energetic charged particles interacting with atmospheric gases, not simply by cosmic rays. 耀斑主要是辐射爆发，日冕物质抛射则涉及大量物质；两者可能同时发生，但并非同一现象。极光主要来自高能带电粒子与大气气体的相互作用，而不是单纯由宇宙射线产生。

03 · Earth's magnetosphere · 地球磁层

Definition: The region of space dominated by Earth's magnetic field, shaped by its interaction with the solar wind. 定义：由地球磁场主导、并受到太阳风影响而形成的空间区域。

1 · Deflects particles

Redirects many incoming charged particles around Earth.

使许多来自太阳的带电粒子偏转，绕过地球。

2 · Protects the atmosphere

Helps reduce direct solar-wind interaction with the atmosphere.

减弱太阳风对地球大气层的直接影响。

3 · Changes shape

Compressed on the Sun-facing side and stretched into a tail away from the Sun.

朝向太阳的一侧受到压缩，背向太阳的一侧形成长尾。

Exam answer / 考试答案: “Earth's magnetosphere protects life by deflecting many harmful charged particles from the solar wind and reducing their direct impact on Earth.”
“地球磁层通过偏转太阳风中的许多有害带电粒子，减少它们对地球的直接影响，从而保护地球生命。”

04 · Space weather & effects on Earth · 太空天气及其影响

Space weather describes changing conditions on the Sun and in near-Earth space that can affect technological systems and the space environment. 太空天气指太阳及近地太空环境的变化，这些变化可能影响科技系统和太空环境。

🌌 Aurora · 极光

Charged particles are guided toward polar regions, where they excite oxygen and nitrogen in the upper atmosphere. The gases emit coloured light.

带电粒子沿磁场进入高纬度地区，激发高层大气中的氧和氮，气体释放彩色光线，形成极光。

📡 Communication · 通信

Solar radiation can disturb the ionosphere, disrupting some radio signals.

太阳辐射可能扰乱电离层，干扰部分无线电通信。

🛰️ Satellites & navigation · 卫星与导航

Energetic particles may damage electronics; ionospheric disturbances can reduce GNSS/GPS accuracy.

高能粒子可能损坏电子设备；电离层扰动可能降低 GPS 等卫星导航精度。

⚡ Power grids · 电网

Geomagnetic storms can induce electrical currents in long conductors, potentially damaging transformers.

地磁暴可能在长距离导体中感应出电流，严重时可能损坏变压器。

Cause → effect / 因果链： Strong solar activity → disturbances in near-Earth space → aurora, radio/GPS disruption, and possible power-grid problems.
强烈太阳活动 → 近地太空环境扰动 → 极光、无线电／GPS 干扰及潜在电网问题。

05 · Exam-ready short answers · 考试答题训练

Q1. State the energy source of the Sun. / 太阳的能量来自哪里？

Answer: Nuclear fusion in the Sun's core converts hydrogen into helium and releases energy.
太阳核心发生核聚变，氢转变成氦并释放能量。

Q2. Why do sunspots look dark? / 为什么太阳黑子看起来较暗？

Answer: Sunspots are cooler than the surrounding photosphere, so they appear darker.
太阳黑子的温度比周围光球层低，因此显得较暗。

Q3. Explain how auroras form. / 解释极光如何形成。

Answer: Energetic charged particles enter Earth's upper atmosphere near the poles and collide with oxygen and nitrogen. Excited gases emit light.
高能带电粒子进入地球两极附近的高层大气，与氧和氮碰撞；受激发的气体释放光线。

Q4. Give two effects of space weather. / 写出太空天气的两个影响。

Answer: (1) Disruption of radio communications or satellite navigation. (2) Induced currents that may disturb electrical power grids.
（1）干扰无线电通信或卫星导航。（2）产生感应电流，可能干扰电网。

Q5. Compare a solar flare with a CME. / 比较耀斑和日冕物质抛射。

Answer: A solar flare is a sudden burst of electromagnetic radiation; a CME ejects a large cloud of magnetised plasma.
太阳耀斑是突然爆发的电磁辐射；日冕物质抛射则将大量带磁场的等离子体抛向太空。

Q6. Why is the magnetosphere important? / 地球磁层为什么重要？

Answer: It deflects many solar-wind charged particles and reduces their direct impact on Earth.
它能偏转太阳风中的许多带电粒子，减少它们对地球的直接影响。

Exam technique / 答题技巧：For “explain” questions, use cause → process → result（原因 → 过程 → 结果）, not only a definition.
