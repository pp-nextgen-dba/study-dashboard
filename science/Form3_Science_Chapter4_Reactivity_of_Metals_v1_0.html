01 • Big picture / 核心概念

Earth's crust contains minerals. Many useful metals occur as compounds in ores. We can extract a metal by removing oxygen from its oxide, but the method depends on how reactive the metal is.

地壳含有矿物。许多有用金属以化合物形式存在于矿石中。要从金属氧化物取得金属，需要去除氧；所用方法取决于金属的反应活性。

Exam logic / 考试逻辑: A metal above carbon in the reactivity series is generally too reactive to be extracted from its oxide by carbon reduction; metals below carbon can often be extracted using carbon or carbon monoxide.活性排在碳以上的金属通常不能用碳还原其氧化物；排在碳以下的金属通常可利用碳或一氧化碳还原。

02 • Minerals: elements vs compounds / 天然元素与天然化合物

Natural elements 天然元素

Contain only one chemical element. Examples: gold (Au), silver (Ag).

只含一种化学元素。例如：金（Au）、银（Ag）。

Natural compounds 天然化合物

Contain two or more elements chemically combined in a fixed composition. Examples: tin(IV) oxide (SnO₂), lead(II) sulphide (PbS).

由两种或更多元素按一定比例化学结合。例如：氧化锡(IV)（SnO₂）、硫化铅(II)（PbS）。

Scientific correction: A compound is a pure substance, not an “impure substance”. Minerals may contain compounds, while real ore samples often contain impurities. Not every natural element is non-reactive.化合物属于纯净物，并非“不纯物质”。实际矿石可能含杂质；天然元素也不一定都不活泼。

03 • Uses of minerals / 矿物的用途

Diamond 金刚石

Very hard → cutting glass and industrial cutting tools.

硬度极高 → 切割玻璃及工业切割工具。

Copper 铜

Good electrical conductor → electrical wires.

导电性好 → 电线。

Marble 大理石

Durable, attractive → tiles, tables and sinks.

坚固且美观 → 瓷砖、桌面和洗手盆。

Calcium oxide 氧化钙

Basic oxide → neutralises acidic soil.

碱性氧化物 → 中和酸性土壤。

Silica 二氧化硅

Heat-resistant material used in glass and some ceramics/cookware.

耐热材料，可用于玻璃、部分陶瓷及厨具。

Hematite 赤铁矿

Important iron ore; the book also mentions magnetic-bracelet products, but medical effectiveness is not established.

重要的铁矿石。课本亦提及磁性手环，但其医疗功效缺乏可靠证据。

04 • Reactivity series / 金属活动性顺序

从最活泼到最不活泼（加入碳和氢作比较）：

Key rule: More reactive metals generally react more vigorously with oxygen under comparable conditions.

关键规律：在相似条件下，金属越活泼，与氧气反应通常越剧烈。

Magnesium 镁

Burns with a dazzling white flame.

燃烧时发出耀眼白光。

2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO

Zinc 锌

Reacts on strong heating to form zinc oxide.

强烈加热时与氧气反应，生成氧化锌。

2Zn + O₂ → 2ZnO

Iron 铁

Heated iron wool can glow or spark in oxygen.

加热的铁丝绒在氧气中可发光或产生火花。

3Fe + 2O₂ → Fe₃O₄

Why can aluminium seem unreactive? / 为什么铝看起来不活泼？

Aluminium rapidly forms a thin, protective aluminium oxide layer. This slows further reaction even though aluminium is high in the series.

铝表面会迅速形成一层致密的氧化铝保护层，使后续反应变慢；但铝本身仍是活泼金属。

Carbon is less reactive than aluminium but more reactive than zinc and lead. Hydrogen is less reactive than zinc but more reactive than iron and lead.

碳的活性低于铝，但高于锌和铅。氢的活性低于锌，但高于铁和铅。

How the experiment proves position / 实验如何证明位置

If carbon removes oxygen from zinc oxide, carbon is acting as the reducing agent. If it cannot reduce aluminium oxide under the experiment's conditions, aluminium is above carbon.

如果碳能从氧化锌中夺走氧，碳便是还原剂。如果在实验条件下无法还原氧化铝，则说明铝的活性高于碳。

2ZnO + C → 2Zn + CO₂

PbO + H₂ → Pb + H₂O

Remember: Reduction means removal of oxygen (at this level). The metal oxide is reduced; carbon, carbon monoxide or hydrogen is oxidised.在本课中，还原可理解为除去氧。金属氧化物被还原，而碳、一氧化碳或氢被氧化。

06 • Iron extraction: blast furnace / 高炉炼铁

Three main raw materials: hematite (iron ore), coke and limestone. Hot air enters near the bottom.

三种主要原料：赤铁矿（铁矿石）、焦炭、石灰石。热空气从高炉下部送入。

1. Produce heat / 产生热量

C + O₂ → CO₂

焦炭燃烧，释放热量。

2. Form reducing gas / 生成还原气体

CO₂ + C → 2CO

二氧化碳与热焦炭反应，生成一氧化碳。

3. Reduce iron oxide / 还原氧化铁

Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

一氧化碳从氧化铁中夺走氧，生成铁。

4. Decompose limestone / 石灰石分解

CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

石灰石受热分解成氧化钙和二氧化碳。

5. Make slag / 形成炉渣

CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃

氧化钙与二氧化硅杂质反应，形成硅酸钙炉渣。

6. Separate products / 分离产物

Slag floats on molten iron because it is less dense. They are tapped separately; iron solidifies on cooling.

炉渣密度较低，因此浮在铁水上方。两者分别排出，铁水冷却后凝固。

Why add limestone? / 为什么加入石灰石？

To remove silica impurities as slag—not primarily to produce iron.

目的是把二氧化硅杂质转化为炉渣，而不是直接制造铁。

07 • Exam answer templates / 考试作答模板

Q: Why can carbon extract iron but not aluminium? / 为什么碳能提炼铁却不能提炼铝？

Carbon is more reactive than iron but less reactive than aluminium. Therefore, carbon can reduce iron oxide but cannot reduce aluminium oxide under ordinary extraction conditions.

碳比铁活泼，但没有铝活泼。因此碳能够还原氧化铁，却无法在一般提炼条件下还原氧化铝。

Q: State the role of coke. / 焦炭有什么作用？

Coke provides heat by combustion and helps produce carbon monoxide, which reduces iron oxide to iron.

焦炭燃烧提供热量，并帮助产生一氧化碳；一氧化碳将氧化铁还原成铁。

Q: Why does slag float? / 为什么炉渣浮在铁水上？

Slag is less dense than molten iron.

炉渣的密度比熔融铁低。

Q: What is the function of limestone? / 石灰石的作用是什么？

Limestone decomposes to calcium oxide, which reacts with silica impurities to form slag.

石灰石分解形成氧化钙，氧化钙与二氧化硅杂质反应生成炉渣。
