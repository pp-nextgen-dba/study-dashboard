What is respiration? 什么是呼吸作用？

Breathing is the movement of air into and out of the lungs. Gaseous exchange is the movement of oxygen and carbon dioxide across respiratory surfaces by diffusion. Cellular respiration is the chemical process in cells that releases energy from glucose.

1. Breathing
呼吸动作

Inhale O₂-rich air; exhale CO₂-rich air.

2. Gas exchange
气体交换

O₂ diffuses into blood; CO₂ diffuses out.

3. Cellular respiration
细胞呼吸

Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy

Core reasoning: Cells need energy → oxygen is supplied by breathing and blood transport → carbon dioxide produced by cells must be removed. 细胞需要能量 → 吸入氧气并由血液运输 → 排出细胞产生的二氧化碳。

Understand why: Larger chest volume → lower pressure → air moves IN. Smaller chest volume → higher pressure → air moves OUT. Air moves from higher to lower pressure. 胸腔体积增大 → 压力降低 → 空气进入；体积减小 → 压力升高 → 空气排出。
Balloon-and-bell-jar model: how it works

Pull the rubber sheet down → jar volume increases → air enters → balloons inflate. Push the sheet up → volume decreases → air leaves → balloons deflate. The balloons represent lungs; rubber sheet represents the diaphragm; jar represents the chest cavity. The rigid jar does not model rib-cage movement.

Movement and Exchange of Gases 气体的运输与交换

Diffusion 扩散: Net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to lower concentration. No energy input is needed for simple diffusion.

At the lungs 在肺部

  • O₂ concentration is higher in alveoli than in deoxygenated blood.
  • O₂ diffuses alveolus → blood.
  • CO₂ concentration is higher in blood than in alveolar air.
  • CO₂ diffuses blood → alveolus and is exhaled.

At body cells 在身体细胞

  • O₂ diffuses blood → cells for cellular respiration.
  • Cells release CO₂ through cellular respiration.
  • CO₂ diffuses cells → blood and returns to lungs.

Haemoglobin transport 血红蛋白运输氧气

Lungs: haemoglobin + oxygen⇌Oxyhaemoglobin 氧合血红蛋白

Oxyhaemoglobin forms in the lungs and releases oxygen near body cells. 氧合血红蛋白在肺部形成，在身体细胞附近释放氧气。

Cellular respiration equation: Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy.
葡萄糖 + 氧气 → 二氧化碳 + 水 + 能量。

Why are alveoli efficient? 肺泡为什么适合气体交换？

AdaptationReason
Millions of alveoli 大量肺泡Large surface area → more diffusion
Walls only one cell thick 壁薄Short diffusion distance → faster diffusion
Moist surfaces 湿润表面Gases dissolve before diffusing

Adaptations in Respiratory Systems 呼吸系统的适应性

Efficient respiratory surfaces generally have large surface area, moist surfaces and thin walls. 有效率的呼吸表面通常具有大表面积、湿润表面和薄壁。

OrganismRespiratory structureKey adaptation / process
Human 人Alveoli 肺泡Millions of thin, moist alveoli surrounded by capillaries
Frog 青蛙Lungs + moist outer skin 肺和湿润皮肤Thin, permeable skin, mucus and dense capillary network; gas exchange through skin and lungs
Fish 鱼Gills 鳃Filaments with many thin lamellae; large area; dissolved O₂ diffuses from water to blood
Insect 昆虫Tracheal system 气管系统Air enters spiracles → tracheae → fine tracheoles → body cells; some have air sacs

Fish gill structure 鱼鳃结构

Mouth→Gills→Operculum opening

Filaments 鳃丝 carry many lamellae 鳃小片, increasing surface area. The operculum 鳃盖 protects the gills and helps move water across them.

Insect tracheal pathway 昆虫气管路径

Spiracle 气门→Trachea 气管→Tracheole 微气管→Body cells

In insects, oxygen is delivered directly to tissues by the tracheal system rather than primarily by blood.

Why do frogs need moist skin?

Oxygen and carbon dioxide must dissolve in moisture before diffusing across the thin skin. Blood capillaries close to the surface transport the gases and help maintain the concentration gradient.

Exam trap: Plants respire both day and night. Photosynthesis usually occurs in light. 植物白天和夜晚都进行细胞呼吸；光合作用需要光。

Stomata and guard cells 气孔与保卫细胞

A stoma is a pore bordered by two guard cells. It allows gas exchange and water loss by transpiration. 气孔由一对保卫细胞围成，可让气体交换，也会通过蒸腾作用失水。

Open stoma 气孔打开Closed stoma 气孔关闭
Water enters guard cells by osmosis 渗透作用Water leaves guard cells by osmosis
Guard cells become turgid 膨胀Guard cells become flaccid 松弛
Cells curve; pore opensPore closes
Usually in daylight with sufficient waterOften at night or during water stress/hot dry conditions

Textbook pattern is a useful general rule, but actual stomatal opening depends on plant species, light, water availability and environment. Some plants open stomata at night.

Osmosis vs diffusion 渗透作用与扩散

Diffusion 扩散

Net movement of particles from high concentration to low concentration.

Example: CO₂ enters leaf through open stomata.

Osmosis 渗透作用

Net movement of water molecules across a partially permeable membrane from higher water potential to lower water potential.

Example: Water enters guard cells and makes them turgid.

PollutantImpact on plants

Exam Answering Guide 考试作答技巧

When asked “Explain”

Give a cause and its effect, not only a keyword.

Weak: “Pressure decreases.”

Strong: “The thoracic cavity increases in volume, so pressure falls below atmospheric pressure and air enters the lungs.”

When asked “Compare”

Use matching features for both sides.

Example: “During inhalation the diaphragm contracts and flattens; during exhalation it relaxes and curves upwards.”
