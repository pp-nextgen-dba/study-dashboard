8.1 Discovery of Radioactivity · 放射性的发现

1895 — Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen

Discovered X-rays while experimenting with a cathode-ray tube. X-rays can pass through soft tissue more readily than bone. Nobel Prize in Physics: 1901.

伦琴发现X射线；X射线较容易穿透软组织，骨骼吸收更多X射线。1901年获诺贝尔物理学奖。

1896 — Henri Becquerel

Discovered spontaneous radiation from uranium salts, which affected a photographic plate even in darkness. Shared the 1903 Nobel Prize in Physics.

贝克勒尔发现铀盐在黑暗中也会自行发出辐射，使感光底片变黑。

1898 — Marie & Pierre Curie

Discovered polonium and radium. Shared the 1903 Nobel Prize in Physics with Becquerel. Marie Curie also won the 1911 Chemistry Nobel Prize.

居里夫妇发现钋和镭。玛丽·居里分别于1903年和1911年获诺贝尔奖。

Textbook accuracy note: The photographed textbook labels the Curie milestone “1897”; the commonly accepted discovery year for polonium and radium is 1898. Also, Marie Curie was the first person to win two Nobel Prizes, not the only woman ever to win two.

What is radioactivity? · 什么是放射性？

Radioactivity is the random and spontaneous decay of an unstable atomic nucleus, accompanied by the emission of radiation.

放射性是不稳定原子核随机、自发地衰变并释放辐射的过程。

Random · 随机

We cannot predict which individual nucleus will decay next.

无法预测哪一个原子核会先衰变。

Spontaneous · 自发

Ordinary changes in temperature and pressure do not control nuclear decay.

一般温度与压力变化不能控制核衰变。

Activity and units · 活度与单位

1 becquerel (Bq) = 1 decay per second
1 curie (Ci) = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq

Half-life · 半衰期

Half-life is the time required for the number of undecayed nuclei (or activity) to fall to half its initial value.

半衰期是未衰变原子核数量（或放射性活度）减少到原来一半所需的时间。

Remaining amount = Initial amount × (½)ⁿ
n = elapsed time ÷ half-life
Visual sequence · 记忆法

100% → 50% → 25% → 12.5% → 6.25%

每经过一个半衰期，剩余量减半。

Worked example · 例题

Po-210: initial 100 g, half-life 3 min, elapsed 9 min.

n = 9 ÷ 3 = 3 → 100 × (½)³ = 12.5 g

Interactive half-life calculator · 半衰期计算器

3 half-lives → 12.5 remaining

8.2 Atom and Nucleus · 原子与原子核

An atom contains a nucleus of protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons. The nucleus holds almost all of the atom’s mass.

原子由质子和中子组成的原子核，以及核外电子构成。原子大部分质量集中在原子核。

Particle 粒子Charge 电荷Location 位置Relative mass 相对质量
Proton 质子+1Nucleus 原子核≈1
Neutron 中子0Nucleus 原子核≈1
Electron 电子−1Outside nucleus 核外≈1/1836
Neutral atom · 中性原子

Number of protons = number of electrons.

Positive ion / Cation · 阳离子

Atom loses electrons. Protons > electrons.

Negative ion / Anion · 阴离子

Atom gains electrons. Electrons > protons.

Exam shortcut: Ion charge = number of protons − number of electrons. Example: 6 protons, 7 electrons → −1 charge. 离子电荷 = 质子数 − 电子数。

8.3 Ionising & Non-ionising Radiation · 电离与非电离辐射

Ionising radiation has sufficient energy to remove electrons from atoms or molecules, forming ions. 电离辐射能从原子或分子中移走电子，形成离子。

Non-ionising radiation does not normally have enough energy per photon to ionise atoms. 非电离辐射通常没有足够的单个光子能量使原子电离。

Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-ray → Gamma
Increasing frequency and photon energy → 频率和光子能量逐渐增加。高能紫外线也可能具有电离能力。

Sources · 辐射来源

Natural 天然

Cosmic rays, radon gas, rocks/soil and naturally occurring radionuclides.

Human-made 人为

Medical imaging and treatment, industrial radioactive sources, nuclear activities.

Radiation measurement · 辐射测量

Bq measures radioactive decay rate (activity). Gy measures absorbed dose (J/kg). Sv measures radiation dose accounting for biological effect.

Bq测量放射性活度；Gy测量吸收剂量；Sv用于反映辐射对人体的生物效应。

A background dose rate of 0.2 µSv/h is a useful textbook comparison, not a universal safety boundary. Actual risk depends on exposure duration, radiation type, dose and circumstances.

8.4 Uses of Radioactive Radiation · 放射性辐射的用途

🏺 Archaeology 考古

Carbon-14 dating estimates the age of once-living materials.

利用碳-14测定曾经存活的有机物的年代。

🏭 Industry 工业

Radiation gauges measure sheet thickness or liquid fill level.

利用辐射检测纸张或金属厚度、瓶内液位。

🌱 Agriculture 农业

Phosphorus-32 as a tracer for fertiliser uptake; pest sterilisation; mutation breeding.

磷-32示踪肥料吸收；害虫绝育；诱变育种。

🍓 Food preservation 食品保鲜

Controlled irradiation reduces microorganisms and pests without making food radioactive.

受控食品辐照可减少微生物与害虫，不会使食物变成放射性物质。

🏥 Medicine 医疗

Co-60: radiotherapy; I-131: thyroid treatment; Tc-99m: diagnostic imaging; Na-24: historical tracer for blood circulation.

钴-60放疗；碘-131治疗甲状腺；锝-99m医学影像；钠-24可作为血液循环示踪剂。

⚠️ Defence 国防

Nuclear weapons involve nuclear reactions and can cause severe blast, heat, radiation and long-term contamination.

核武器可造成爆炸、热辐射、核辐射及长期污染。

Safety principles · 安全原则

⏱️ Time 时间

Minimise exposure time. 缩短暴露时间。

📏 Distance 距离

Increase distance from source. 远离辐射源。

🧱 Shielding 屏蔽

Use suitable shielding and protective equipment. 使用合适的屏蔽物与防护装备。

Additional precautions: use medical X-rays only when justified; follow radiation-worker monitoring protocols; prevent ingestion of contaminated food; store and dispose of radioactive waste through authorised procedures.

其他措施：仅在医疗需要时进行X射线检查；辐射工作人员接受剂量监测；避免食用受污染食品；放射性废物须依照规定储存和处置。

Science correction: Ordinary televisions are not generally a significant ionising-radiation source. UV protection is important for sunlight exposure, but it should not be confused with shielding against alpha, beta, gamma or X-rays.

Exam-answering technique · 答题技巧

Definition: Include the key words “random”, “spontaneous”, “unstable nucleus”, and “emits radiation”.

Comparison: State a paired difference, e.g. “Alpha has high ionising power but low penetrating power, whereas gamma has low ionising power but high penetrating power.”

Calculation: Show formula → substitution → answer with unit. For half-life, first calculate the number of half-lives.

定义题抓关键词；比较题要同时写出两者差异；计算题写公式、代入、答案与单位。
