🧠 1.1 Human Nervous System 人体神经系统

Central Nervous System (CNS)中枢神经系统

Brain 脑 — protected by skull 受颅骨保护; coordinates body activities 协调身体活动。

Spinal cord 脊髓 — protected by backbone 受脊柱保护; coordinates many reflexes 协调许多反射动作。

Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)周围神经系统

12 pairs of cranial nerves / 12 对脑神经

31 pairs of spinal nerves / 31 对脊神经

Connect CNS with the rest of the body / 连接中枢神经系统与身体其他部分。

👁 Structure of the Eye 眼睛的结构与功能

Rod cells vs Cone cells / 视杆细胞与视锥细胞

🌙 Rod cells · 视杆细胞

Detect dim light and light intensity; do not detect colour.感受微弱光线及明暗，不辨别颜色。

🌈 Cone cells · 视锥细胞

Colour vision, work best in bright light.辨别颜色，在强光下效果最佳。

Yellow spot 黄斑 — sharpest central vision / 中央视觉最清晰；Blind spot 盲点 — no photoreceptors / 没有感光细胞。

👃 👅 ✋ Smell, Taste & Skin 嗅觉、味觉与皮肤

Smell · 嗅觉

Odour molecules dissolve in mucus → stimulate smell receptors → impulses travel to brain.气味分子溶于黏液 → 刺激嗅觉感受器 → 神经冲动传至大脑。

Taste · 味觉

Chemicals dissolve in saliva → enter taste pores → stimulate receptors → brain interprets taste.物质溶于唾液 → 进入味孔 → 刺激味觉感受器 → 大脑识别味道。

Five tastes: sweet 甜, sour 酸, salty 咸, bitter 苦, umami 鲜。

Skin · 皮肤

Five receptor types: touch 触觉, pressure 压力, heat 热, cold 冷, pain 痛。

More receptors + thinner epidermis generally mean greater touch sensitivity.感受器越密集、表皮越薄，触觉一般越敏感。

Important / 重要：All regions of the tongue can detect all five basic tastes. 舌头各区域都能感受五种基本味道。

🔍 Limits of sight · 视觉限制

Tiny objects, very distant objects, optical illusions and blind spots.微小物体、远距离物体、视觉错觉、盲点。

Tools: microscope, binoculars, telescope, periscope and medical imaging.辅助工具：显微镜、双筒望远镜、天文望远镜、潜望镜、医学成像设备。

🦻 Hearing limitations · 听觉限制

Hearing aids, stethoscopes, speakers and megaphones can help amplify or convey sounds.助听器、听诊器、扬声器和扩音器可帮助放大或传递声音。

⚕️ Hearing damage · 听觉损伤

Causes: ageing, infection, injury, prolonged loud noise.原因：老化、感染、受伤、长期噪声暴露。

Care depends on the damaged part: obstruction removal, medicines/surgery, or cochlear implants for selected severe inner-ear hearing loss.处理方式取决于受损部位：清除堵塞、药物／手术，或在适当情况下植入人工耳蜗。

🛡️ Protect your senses · 保护感官

Rest eyes regularly (20-20-20), keep a comfortable screen distance, adjust brightness, and use hearing protection in loud environments.定时让眼睛休息（20-20-20法则）、保持合适屏幕距离、调整亮度，并在嘈杂环境使用听力保护装备。

🌱 Stimuli and Responses in Plants 植物的刺激与反应

Tropism · 向性运动

Directional growth response; usually slow. Positive = towards stimulus; negative = away.植物朝刺激方向或背离刺激方向生长，通常较慢。正向性＝朝向；负向性＝背离。

Nastic movement · 感性运动

Movement independent of stimulus direction, often faster; e.g. Mimosa leaves fold when touched.运动方向不取决于刺激方向，通常较快，例如含羞草叶子受触碰后闭合。

Type 类型Stimulus 刺激Shoot 茎／芽Root 根Why? 原因
Phototropism 向光性Light 光Positive 正Typically negative 通常负Shoots access light for photosynthesis 茎获得光线进行光合作用
Hydrotropism 向水性Water 水Typically negative 通常负Positive 正Roots absorb water and minerals 根吸收水与矿物质
Geotropism 向地性Gravity 重力Negative 负Positive 正Roots anchor plant; shoots grow upwards 根固定植物；茎向上生长
Thigmotropism 向触性Touch 接触Tendrils coil around support · 卷须缠绕支架Climbing plants reach light 攀缘植物获得阳光

Memory trick / 记忆口诀： Photo = light 光 · Hydro = water 水 · Geo = Earth 地 · Thigmo = touch 触。 Root: water + gravity → positive. 根：水与重力→正向。

👂 Stereophonic hearing · 双耳立体听觉

The ear nearer a sound source usually receives it earlier and louder. The brain compares arrival time and loudness to locate the source.靠近声源的耳朵通常较早、较响地接收到声音；大脑比较时间差与响度差来判断方向。

Survival adaptations · 生存适应

🦋 Pheromones · 信息素

Insects use chemical signals to attract mates, warn others and find food.昆虫利用化学信号吸引配偶、发出警告及寻找食物。

🐟 Lateral line · 侧线

Fish detect vibrations and movement in surrounding water.鱼类通过侧线感知周围水的振动与流动。

⚡ Electric eel · 电鳗

Produces electric discharges to stun prey and defend itself.释放电流击晕猎物并保护自己。
