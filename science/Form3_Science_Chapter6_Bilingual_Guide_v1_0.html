00 · Big picture / 整体概念

Why are we learning this chapter?

Electricity is an energy carrier. A power station converts energy from a source into electrical energy. The grid delivers it to us. Appliances convert electrical energy into light, heat, sound or movement.

电是一种能量传递方式。发电站把其他形式的能量转化为电能，电网将电能送到用户，电器再把电能转化为光、热、声音或运动。

Energy source 能源→Power station 发电站→Transmission 输电→Home 家庭→Appliance 电器
Core idea / 核心： Energy is transferred and transformed, not created from nothing. 能量只会转移或转换，不会凭空产生。
01 · Sources / 能源来源

Renewable vs non-renewable energy

♻️ Renewable 可再生能源

Replenished naturally on a human timescale.

能在相对较短时间内由自然过程补充。

Examples: Solar, wind, hydro, tidal, wave, geothermal, biomass.

太阳能、风能、水力、潮汐能、波浪能、地热能、生物质能。

⛽ Non-renewable 不可再生能源

Finite resources that are depleted faster than they are replaced.

储量有限，消耗速度远快于自然形成速度。

Examples: Petroleum, natural gas, coal and nuclear fuel (uranium).

石油、天然气、煤炭，以及核燃料（铀）。

Exam distinction: Renewable does not mean zero environmental impact; biomass can still release CO₂ when burned. Nuclear energy is low-carbon in operation but classified as non-renewable because uranium is finite.

考试重点：可再生不等于完全没有环境影响；核能运行时碳排放低，但铀矿有限，所以属于不可再生能源。

02 · Generation / 发电原理

How four power stations work

💧 Hydroelectric 水力发电

Reservoir water flows down → spins turbine → generator produces electricity.

水库的水从高处流下 → 推动涡轮机 → 发电机产生电能。

Gravitational potential → kinetic → electrical

More precise: moving water turns a turbine (mechanical energy), which drives the generator.

🌱 Biomass 生物质发电

Biomass-derived methane burns → heats water → steam spins turbine → generator.

燃烧生物质产生的甲烷 → 加热水 → 蒸汽推动涡轮机 → 发电。

Chemical → heat → kinetic → electrical

🔥 Thermal 火力发电

Coal, diesel or natural gas burns → steam → turbine → generator.

燃烧煤、柴油或天然气 → 产生蒸汽 → 推动涡轮机 → 发电。

Chemical → heat → kinetic → electrical

☢️ Nuclear 核能发电

Nuclear fission releases heat → steam → turbine → generator.

核裂变释放热能 → 产生蒸汽 → 推动涡轮机 → 发电。

Nuclear → heat → kinetic → electrical
Memory shortcut: Three of these use heat → steam → turbine → generator. Hydroelectric uses moving water directly, without needing steam.

记忆技巧：生物质、火力、核能都靠热量产生蒸汽；水力发电直接利用流动的水。

Exam: Why does a turbine matter? / 涡轮机有什么作用？

A turbine converts the energy of flowing water or steam into rotational mechanical energy to drive a generator.

涡轮机将流动的水或蒸汽的能量转换为旋转机械能，带动发电机。

03 · Current / 电流类型

Direct current (DC) vs alternating current (AC)

DC · 直流电

Current flows in one direction.

电流只沿一个方向流动。

Examples: battery, solar cell; battery-powered torch.

AC · 交流电

Current reverses direction periodically.

电流方向会周期性反转。

Examples: mains electricity from power stations.

Why AC for the grid? Transformers can easily step AC voltage up or down, allowing efficient long-distance transmission.

为什么电网使用交流电？变压器可以方便地升高或降低交流电压，适合长距离输电。

A normal transformer requires changing magnetic flux; steady DC does not produce continuous transformer action. Modern electronic converters can change DC voltage, but that is different from an ordinary transformer.

普通变压器需要变化的磁通量；稳定直流电无法持续通过普通变压器升降压。

04 · Transformers / 变压器

Step-up and step-down transformers

Two coils (primary input and secondary output) are wound around a laminated soft-iron core. Alternating current creates changing magnetic flux, inducing voltage in the secondary coil.

变压器由初级线圈、次级线圈及叠片软铁芯组成。交流电产生变化的磁通量，在次级线圈感应出电压。

Vp / Vs = Np / Ns   (ideal transformer)

⬆ Step-up 升压

Secondary turns more than primary: Ns > Np → Vs > Vp. Output current is lower in the ideal case.

次级匝数更多 → 输出电压更高；理想情况下输出电流更小。

⬇ Step-down 降压

Secondary turns fewer than primary: Ns < Np → Vs < Vp. Output current is higher in the ideal case.

次级匝数更少 → 输出电压更低；理想情况下输出电流更大。

Worked example / 例题

Vp = 240 V, Np = 600, Ns = 150. Vs = 240 × 150 / 600 = 60 V. Step-down transformer.

输出电压为 60 V，属于降压变压器。

05 · Transmission / 输配电

From power station to home

Power station
11 / 25 kV→Step-up
132 / 275 / 500 kV→National Grid→Step-down substations→Home ~240 V

Why increase voltage? For the same transmitted power, higher voltage means lower current. Since wire heating loss is approximately I²R, lower current reduces wasted energy.

为什么先升压？输送相同功率时，电压越高，电流越小。电线发热损失约为 I²R，所以降低电流能减少能量浪费。

National Grid Network 国家电网

High-voltage transmission lines connecting generation and demand regions.

连接发电站与用电地区的高压输电网络。

Switch zone 开关站

Allows sections of the grid to be isolated for maintenance and switching operations.

在维修或调度时隔离部分电网。

Step-down station 降压变电站

Reduces voltage to suitable levels for factories, buildings and homes.

将电压降低至工厂、建筑物和家庭适用的等级。

The textbook illustrates 240 V for domestic supply. Actual nominal household supply in Malaysia is commonly specified as 230 V (single-phase); exam questions should use the voltage stated in the question.

课本使用 240 V 作例子；实际马来西亚单相家用标称电压通常为 230 V。考试以题目给定数值计算。

06 · Home wiring / 家庭用电安全

Three wires, protective devices and plugs

🟤 Live 火线

Brown. Supplies electrical potential to the appliance. Dangerous to touch.

棕色。向电器提供电压，触碰可能触电。

🔵 Neutral 中性线

Blue. Normal return path for current.

蓝色。电流在正常电路中的返回路径。

🟢🟡 Earth 地线

Green/yellow. Safety connection for exposed conductive cases; normally carries no current.

黄绿色。连接电器金属外壳以保护使用者；正常情况下不载流。

Component 元件Function 功能Remember 记忆点
Fuse 保险丝Melts to break circuit when current is too high. / 电流过大时熔断。Single-use / 一次性
MCB 微型断路器Trips on overload or short circuit. / 过载或短路时跳闸。Resettable / 可复位
ELCB / RCD 漏电保护器Trips on earth leakage; modern RCDs detect live–neutral current imbalance. / 检测漏电并断电。Shock protection / 防触电
Earth wire 地线Provides a low-resistance fault path for metal-cased appliances. / 为故障电流提供安全路径。Protection / 保护
Main switch 总开关Isolates house supply. / 切断房屋电源。Isolation / 隔离
Lightning conductor 避雷针Conducts lightning current to earth. / 将雷电流导入大地。Lightning / 雷电

3-pin vs 2-pin / 三脚与两脚插头

3-pin: live, neutral, earth; typically used for appliances requiring protective earthing, such as some metal-cased kettles and irons. 2-pin: live and neutral; often used for double-insulated appliances that do not require earth.

三脚插头：火线、中性线、地线；常用于需要接地保护的电器。两脚插头：火线与中性线；常用于双重绝缘、不需要接地的电器。

Safety: Never touch exposed wires, bypass earth connections, or repair mains wiring without a qualified electrician. Fuse selection must follow appliance and plug specifications; do not simply choose the highest rating.

安全提醒：不要接触裸露电线、取消接地保护或自行维修市电线路。保险丝应按设备规格选用，并非越大越好。

Exam: MCB vs ELCB / 两者有何不同？

MCB protects against excessive current (overload/short circuit). An earth-leakage protective device protects against leakage to earth; it is not a substitute for overcurrent protection.

MCB 主要防过电流；漏电保护器主要防漏电，两者作用不同。

Efficiency 效率Useful energy / input energy × 100%%
Ideal transformer 理想变压器Vp / Vs = Np / NsRatio / 比例
Unit conversions / 单位换算: 1 kW = 1000 W; 1 h = 3600 s; 1 min = 60 s; 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J.

注意：用焦耳计算时采用 W 和秒；用电费计算时采用 kW 和小时。

Worked example A: current / 电流

A 3.2 kW refrigerator is rated 240 V. Find current.

I = P / V = 3200 / 240 = 13.33 A

先将 3.2 kW 转为 3200 W，再代入公式。注意这里采用课本的简化 P = VI 模型。

Worked example B: energy / 能量

3.0 kW hair dryer for 20 min. Find energy in joules.

E = Pt = 3000 × (20 × 60) = 3,600,000 J

Worked example C: electricity bill / 电费

2.5 kW rice cooker, 15 minutes, RM0.20 per kWh.

E = 2.5 × 0.25 = 0.625 kWh
Cost = 0.625 × RM0.20 = RM0.125 ≈ RM0.13

15 分钟 = 0.25 小时。先求耗电量，再乘每度电的价格。这里的电价只是例题，不代表当前实际电费。

09 · Exam-ready / 考试冲刺

Last-minute memory sheet

1. Energy sources

Renewable replenishes; non-renewable is finite.

可再生可补充；不可再生储量有限。

2. Generator

Turbine rotates generator → electrical energy.

涡轮机带动发电机产生电能。

3. AC vs DC

AC reverses; DC one direction.

交流电反向；直流电单向。

4. Transformers

More secondary turns → higher output voltage.

次级线圈匝数越多，输出电压越高。

5. National Grid

High voltage → lower current → less I²R loss.

高电压降低电流，从而减少输电损失。

6. Protection

Fuse/MCB: overcurrent. ELCB/RCD: leakage.

保险丝/MCB 防过电流；漏电保护器防漏电。

7. Electrical energy

kWh = kW × hours.

度电 = 千瓦 × 小时。

8. Efficiency

Useful output ÷ total input × 100%.

有用输出 ÷ 总输入 × 100%。

Common mistakes / 常见失分点

  • Using minutes directly in a kWh calculation instead of converting to hours. / 没把分钟换成小时。
  • Using kW instead of W in a joule calculation. / 计算焦耳时没将 kW 转为 W。
  • Confusing step-up voltage with step-up current. / 误以为升压也一定升流。
  • Confusing earth leakage with overload protection. / 混淆漏电与过载保护。
  • Claiming energy is destroyed rather than converted to less useful forms. / 误以为能量消失。

Exam answer patterns / 考试作答模板

Why is voltage stepped up before long-distance transmission?

To reduce the current for the same transmitted power, thereby reducing heat loss in transmission cables.

在输送相同功率时降低电流，从而减少输电线中的热能损失。

Why is an earth wire important for a metal-cased appliance?

It provides a low-resistance path for fault current to flow to earth, allowing protective devices to disconnect the supply and reducing electric-shock risk.

发生漏电时提供低电阻路径，让保护装置切断电源，降低触电风险。

How do you solve a 3-step electricity-cost question?

1. Convert W to kW. 2. Convert minutes to hours. 3. Compute energy = kW × h, then cost = energy × tariff.

第一步将瓦换成千瓦；第二步将分钟换成小时；第三步先算度数，再乘电价。
