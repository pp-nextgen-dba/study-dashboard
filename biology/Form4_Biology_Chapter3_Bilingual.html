Big picture · 核心概念

Why do cells need a plasma membrane?

ENGLISHThe membrane acts as a selectively permeable boundary. Cells must take in nutrients and oxygen, release wastes, and maintain the right internal conditions. Different substances use different transport mechanisms.
中文解释细胞膜是具有选择透过性的边界。细胞必须吸收营养和氧气、排出废物，并维持稳定的内部环境。不同物质需要通过不同的运输方式进出细胞。
Membrane structure→Transport mechanism→Cell survival
Exam thinking / 考试思路： For any movement question ask: What moves? Which direction? Through what? Is ATP required?
先问：什么物质移动？朝哪个方向？经过什么结构？需要 ATP 吗？
3.1

Structure of the Plasma Membrane

Fluid Mosaic Model · 流动镶嵌模型 · Singer & Nicolson, 1972

ENGLISHPhospholipids have hydrophilic heads that face watery surroundings and hydrophobic tails that face inward, away from water. Proteins are embedded in a flexible bilayer; this is why it is called a fluid mosaic.
中文解释磷脂具有亲水头部（朝向水）和疏水尾部（朝内、避开水）。不同蛋白质镶嵌在可流动的磷脂双层中，因此称为“流动镶嵌模型”。
Component / 结构Function / 功能
Phospholipid bilayer
磷脂双层		Barrier to many ions and polar molecules; small nonpolar molecules pass more easily.
阻挡许多离子与极性分子；小型非极性分子较容易通过。
Channel protein
通道蛋白		Forms selective passages for certain ions or water molecules.
提供特定离子或水分子的选择性通道。
Carrier protein
载体蛋白		Binds specific solutes and changes shape to transport them.
结合特定物质并改变形状完成运输。
Cholesterol
胆固醇		Regulates fluidity and stabilises animal cell membranes.
调节流动性并稳定动物细胞膜。
Glycoprotein / Glycolipid
糖蛋白／糖脂		Important for cell recognition, signalling and adhesion.
参与细胞识别、讯号传递和黏附。
Memory trick: Heads Hug water; tails Turn away. / 头部亲水，尾部避水。
3.2

Passive vs Active Transport

PASSIVE TRANSPORT · 被动运输Net movement down a concentration gradient (higher → lower concentration). Does not require ATP. Includes simple diffusion, facilitated diffusion and osmosis.
被动运输物质顺浓度梯度净移动（高浓度 → 低浓度），不需要 ATP。包括简单扩散、协助扩散和渗透作用。
ACTIVE TRANSPORT · 主动运输Transport against a concentration/electrochemical gradient using energy, directly or indirectly from ATP. Example: sodium–potassium pump.
主动运输物质逆浓度或电化学梯度移动，需要能量（直接或间接来自 ATP）。例子：钠钾泵。

Three passive transport mechanisms / 三种被动运输

MechanismHow it worksExample
Simple diffusion
简单扩散		Directly through lipid bilayer, high → low.
直接穿过磷脂双层，高 → 低。		O₂ and CO₂ gas exchange / 气体交换
Facilitated diffusion
协助扩散		High → low using a channel/carrier; no ATP.
通过通道／载体顺梯度移动，不耗 ATP。		Glucose via suitable carriers; ions via channels / 葡萄糖或离子
Osmosis
渗透作用		Net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from higher water potential to lower water potential.
水通过选择透过性膜，从较高水势向较低水势净移动。		Water enters root hair cells / 根毛细胞吸水

Visualise concentration gradients / 浓度梯度示意

How the sodium–potassium pump works / 钠钾泵步骤

3 Na⁺ bind inside→ATP phosphorylates pump→3 Na⁺ out→2 K⁺ bind outside→2 K⁺ in
WHY ATP? The pump moves Na⁺ out and K⁺ in against their electrochemical gradients. ATP supplies energy for shape changes. One cycle typically uses one ATP and transports 3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in.
为什么需要 ATP？ 钠钾泵使 Na⁺ 排出、K⁺ 进入，均逆电化学梯度运输。ATP 提供蛋白质改变形状所需的能量。每循环通常消耗 1 个 ATP，排出 3 个 Na⁺、带入 2 个 K⁺。
Exam trap / 常见陷阱： Facilitated diffusion uses a protein but does not require ATP. The presence of a protein does not automatically mean active transport.
协助扩散虽然需要蛋白质，但不消耗 ATP；“经过蛋白质”不等于主动运输。

许多植物韧皮部蔗糖装载涉及主动运输，之后主要靠压力流动运输；不能把整个过程都当作直接消耗 ATP 的泵送。

Hypotonic, Hypertonic & Isotonic Solutions

低渗、高渗、等渗溶液对植物和动物细胞的影响

SolutionPlant cell / 植物细胞Animal cell / 动物细胞
Hypotonic
低渗
Outside has lower effective solute concentration		Water enters → vacuole expands → turgid (膨胀／坚挺)Water enters → swells; red blood cells may undergo haemolysis (溶血)
Hypertonic
高渗
Outside has higher effective solute concentration		Water leaves → protoplast shrinks → plasmolysis (质壁分离)Water leaves → shrinks → crenation (皱缩)
Isotonic
等渗		No net water movement → typically flaccid (松弛)No net water movement → maintains normal shape (维持形状)

Cell response illustration / 细胞变化图

WHY PLANT CELLS DO NOT BURST EASILYThe rigid cell wall resists further expansion and builds turgor pressure as water enters. Animal cells lack a cell wall, so excessive water uptake may cause lysis.
为什么植物细胞不容易胀破？坚硬的细胞壁限制进一步膨胀，吸水时产生膨压。动物细胞没有细胞壁，因此大量吸水可能造成细胞破裂。
Three-word recall / 三词速记： Hypo → water in; Hyper → water out; Iso → no net movement. / 低渗进水、高渗出水、等渗无净移动。
3.4

Applications in Daily Life

Why plants wilt / 植物为什么萎蔫？

Dry soil / Too much fertiliser→Water leaves cells→Loss of turgor→Wilting
EXPLANATIONDrought reduces soil water availability. Excess fertiliser can make the soil solution more concentrated, lowering its water potential. Water may leave root cells by osmosis; loss of turgor leads to wilting. Watering can restore turgor if cells are still viable.
解释干旱使土壤缺水。施肥过量会提高土壤溶液浓度，降低水势，水可能通过渗透作用离开根细胞。细胞失去膨压，植物便萎蔫。若细胞仍存活，适当浇水可恢复膨压。
Application / 应用Why it matters / 原理
Isotonic drinks / 等渗运动饮料Replace water, electrolytes and often carbohydrate after exercise; formulations vary. / 补充水分、电解质及部分糖分。
Normal saline / 生理盐水0.9% NaCl is approximately isotonic with blood; appropriate sterile solutions are used in clinical care. / 0.9% 氯化钠溶液与血液大致等渗；医疗用途须使用适当无菌制剂。
Oral rehydration solution / 口服补液盐Glucose–sodium cotransport helps intestinal water absorption during diarrhoea. / 葡萄糖与钠协同吸收，促进肠道吸水。
Liposomes / 脂质体Lipid vesicles can encapsulate substances and help deliver certain medicines; not every formulation survives digestion or reaches a target cell. / 脂质囊泡可包裹物质，帮助部分药物递送，但并非所有制剂都能经消化后抵达目标细胞。
SPM Answering Skills

How to Score in Structured Questions

Model question 1 · 模拟题一

A red blood cell is placed in distilled water. Explain what happens. (3 marks)
红血球放入蒸馏水中会发生什么？解释原因。（3分）

  1. Distilled water is hypotonic relative to the cell. / 蒸馏水相对细胞为低渗溶液。
  2. Water enters the cell by osmosis down the water potential gradient. / 水沿水势梯度经渗透作用进入细胞。
  3. The cell swells and may burst (haemolysis) because it lacks a cell wall. / 红血球因无细胞壁而膨胀，可能发生溶血。

Model question 2 · 模拟题二

Explain why mineral ions can enter root hair cells when their concentration is higher inside the cell. (3 marks)

  1. Mineral ions move against the concentration gradient. / 离子逆浓度梯度移动。
  2. Specific carrier proteins transport the ions. / 特定载体蛋白运输离子。
  3. Energy from ATP is required for active transport. / 主动运输需要 ATP 提供能量。
Command words / 指令词： State = give a fact (陈述); Explain = cause + mechanism + outcome (原因＋机制＋结果); Compare = similarities and differences (比较相同与不同); Predict = expected outcome using scientific principles (根据原理预测)。
