1.1 Fields and Careers in Biology

生物学的领域与职业

Core definitionBiology is the scientific study of life, living organisms, and their interactions with each other and the environment.
核心定义生物学是以科学方法研究生命、生物体，以及生物之间和生物与环境之间相互作用的学科。
Think about it / 想一想
Why do leaves turn towards light? Why do infections spread? Why do children resemble their parents? These are different biological questions.
为什么叶子会朝向光？为什么感染会传播？为什么孩子与父母相似？这些都是不同的生物学问题。

1.2 Safety and Rules in Biology Laboratory

生物实验室安全与规则

PrincipleLaboratory rules protect people, specimens and the environment. Choose protection according to the hazard; never assume all waste is safe to discard.
基本原则实验室规则保护人员、实验材料与环境。应根据危险种类选择防护措施，不能假设所有废物都可以随意丢弃。

Personal protective equipment / 个人防护装备

🥼 Worn by students / 学生穿戴

Laboratory coat, suitable closed shoes, gloves, goggles and face mask as required by the activity.

实验袍、包脚鞋；按实验风险使用手套、护目镜及口罩。

🚿 Safety facilities / 安全设施

Eyewash, emergency shower, handwashing facilities, fume hood and biological safety cabinet. A laminar-flow clean bench is not automatically a biological safety cabinet.

洗眼器、紧急冲淋设备、洗手设施、通风橱及生物安全柜。普通层流洁净工作台不等同于生物安全柜。

Waste disposal / 废物处理

Type / 类型Correct approach / 正确方法
Sharps 锐利废物Use an approved puncture-resistant sharps container / 放入专用防刺穿锐器容器
Non-sharp biological waste 非锐利生物废物Place in suitable labelled biohazard containment; decontaminate as directed / 使用标示清楚的生物危害容器，依规定消毒
Animal carcasses 动物尸体Use designated biohazard containment and authorised storage/disposal / 依规定包装、储存与处理
Biological liquids 生物液体Decontaminate using an approved procedure before authorised disposal / 按批准程序消毒后处理
Organic solvents, toxic/heavy-metal/reactive chemicals 有机溶剂、有毒/重金属/反应性化学品Never pour into sink; segregate and follow school waste procedures / 不可倒入水槽，必须分类处理
Important exam vs real-life distinction / 考试与实际操作
Your reference lists some mild aqueous solutions (such as sucrose or starch solution) as potentially sink-disposable. In practice, disposal requires teacher approval and local laboratory rules; pH 5–9 alone does not make a substance safe.
参考书把某些温和水溶液（如蔗糖、淀粉溶液）列为可排入水槽的例子。但实际操作必须遵从教师与学校规定；仅凭 pH 5–9 不能判断物质是否安全。

What to do after a spill / 泄漏事故应对

Alert the teacher immediately / 立即通知教师Do not attempt to clean an unknown spill yourself. 不要自行清理未知物质。
Keep others away / 隔离现场Prevent exposure and restrict access. 防止他人接触危险区域。
Identify the hazard / 判断危险种类Only trained personnel should decide containment and cleanup methods. 由受过训练的人员决定围堵与清理方法。
Use the appropriate spill procedure / 按程序处理Mercury and other hazardous spills require specialist handling; do not touch or sweep them. 水银等危险泄漏须由专业人员处理，不可触碰或扫除。
Dispose safely and report / 安全处置并记录Follow teacher instructions and emergency procedures. 遵照教师指示及紧急程序。
Safety correction / 安全补充： The reference book mentions sulphur for mercury spills. Do not use sulphur or attempt a mercury cleanup yourself; evacuate the area and notify trained staff. 参考书提及使用硫处理水银泄漏。学生不应自行使用硫或清理水银，应远离现场并通知受训人员。

生物学中的沟通与资料呈现

Main ideaScientific information must be accurate, clearly labelled and easy for others to interpret.
核心概念科学资料必须准确、标示清楚，并且容易让其他人理解。

Biological drawing / 生物绘图

Use a sharp pencil, clear continuous lines, accurate proportions, a title, and straight non-crossing label lines. Avoid shading unless instructed. Include magnification or scale when required.

1.4 Scientific Investigation in Biology

生物学的科学探究

Scientific methodA systematic process of asking questions, testing predictions, collecting evidence and drawing justified conclusions.
科学方法有系统地提出问题、验证预测、收集证据，并根据结果得出有根据的结论。

Eight steps / 八个科学探究步骤

Understand variables / 理解变量

Manipulated variable / 操纵性变数

The factor you deliberately change.

实验者主动改变的因素。

Responding variable / 反应性变数

The result you measure or observe.

实验中测量或观察的结果。

Controlled variable / 固定性变数

Factors kept constant for a fair test.

为了公平比较而保持不变的因素。

Hypothesis / 假设

A testable prediction relating variables.

关于变量关系、能够通过实验检验的预测。

Worked example: light and photosynthesis / 光照与光合作用

Problem / 问题： How does light intensity affect the rate of photosynthesis in an aquatic plant?光照强度如何影响水生植物的光合作用速率？
Hypothesis / 假设： As light intensity increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases within the tested range, provided other factors are not limiting.在测试范围内，若其他因素不构成限制，光照强度增加时，光合作用速率提高。
VariableExampleExplanation / 解释
Manipulated 操纵Light intensity 光照强度Change the measured light intensity / 改变并测量光照强度
Responding 反应Photosynthesis rate 光合作用速率Measure oxygen produced per minute; bubble count is only a rough proxy / 测量每分钟氧气产量；气泡数仅为粗略指标
Controlled 固定Plant species, temperature, CO₂ supply, observation timeKeep species, temperature, CO₂ supply and timing the same / 保持植物种类、温度、二氧化碳供应及时间一致
Exam reasoning / 考试思路： A valid conclusion must follow the actual data. A hypothesis is not automatically correct, and repeated trials improve reliability.结论必须根据实际数据。假设不一定正确；重复实验可以提高结果的可靠性。
